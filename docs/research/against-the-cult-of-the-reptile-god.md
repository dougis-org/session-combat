# Against the Cult of the Reptile God — Research

## Adventure structure

N1 module (1982) by Douglas Niles, the first published adventure in the N (Novice) series. 1e AD&D. Levels 1–3. Set in Greyhawk. Niles' first published adventure (he wrote it 4 weeks after joining TSR). Famous as a low-level mystery: party arrives in the village of Orlane, investigates disappearances, and confronts the Reptile God cult in a dungeon cave complex.

For G5 we treat N1 as a **3-chapter sampling**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Village of Orlane Investigation | 1–2 | Village of Orlane (social/investigation) |
| 2 | The Wilderness Foraging Expedition | 2 | Wastelands around Orlane |
| 3 | The Reptile God Cult's Cave Dungeon | 2–3 | Cult's cave complex (final confrontation) |

## Encounter plan (3-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — Village of Orlane
- **The Stalked Orlane Mystery.** Villagers are secretly charmed by the cult; some are friendly, some distant, some hostile. Monsters: **CM-orlane-charmed-villager** (custom CR-0 commoner under charm), **CM-reptile-cult-rogue** (custom CR-2 humanoid cult scout).

### Ch 2 — The Wilderness Expedition
- **Wastelands Encounter.** Troglodytes in a maze leading to the cult's stronghold. Notable map error: the troglodyte maze has no exit. Monsters: **CM-troglodyte-cult-guard** (SRD troglodyte analog), giant lizard (SRD), **CM-explictica-defilus` actually Explictica Defilus** (CR-7 yuan-ti-touched priestess).

### Ch 3 — Cult's Cave Dungeon (Reptile God Lair)
- **Reptile God Cult Final Battle.** Explictica Defilus, self-described "Reptile God," and her reptilian servitors. Monsters: **CM-explictica-defilus** (custom CR-9 yuan-ti/medusa hybrid priestess), yuan-ti purebloods (SRD), giant constrictor snakes (SRD), giant poisonous snakes (SRD).
