# Savage Tide — Research

## Adventure structure

12-chapter *Dungeon* magazine Adventure Path (October 2006 – September 2007, issues #139–150), running levels 1–20. Set nominally in Greyhawk but built around Sasserine (just north of Cauldron from Shackled City) and the Isle of Dread (classic Mystara setting). Designed to be easily reskinnable to Forgotten Realms or Eberron.

| # | Chapter | Author | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | There Is No Honor | James Jacobs | 1–3 | Sasserine (murderous feud) |
| 2 | The Bullywug Gambit | Nicolas Logue | 3–5 | Sasserine outskirts (Savage Tide) |
| 3 | The Sea Wyvern's Wake | Richard Pett | 5–7 | Sea voyage (Tamoachan hints) |
| 4 | Here There Be Monsters | Jason Bulmahn | 7–8 | Isle of Dread (shipwreck) |
| 5 | Tides of Dread | Greer / Holian | 9–10 | Isle of Dread (Farshore colony) |

| 7 | City of Broken Idols | Tito Leati | 13–15 | Isle of Dread (taboo plateau) |
| 8 | Serpents of Scuttlecove | Jacobs / Pett | 15–17 | Scuttlecove (pirate city) |
| 9 | Into the Maw | Robert J. Schwalb | 17–18 | Abyss (prison fortress) |
| 10 | Wells of Darkness | Eric L. Boyd | 18–19 | Wells of Darkness (Shami-Amourae) |

| 12 | Prince of Demons | Greg A. Vaughan | 20 | Gaping Maw (Demogorgon's realm) |

Final villain: **Demogorgon**, demon prince, attempting to activate the Savage Tide and drive the world insane. Allied with the demon-princeess **Demogorgon's daughter Ibieahl** (desperate for her father's approval).

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — There Is No Honor
- **House Lyrandar Murder Mystery in Sasserine.** Undead pirates and a thieves' guild are tearing the noble houses apart. Monsters: skeletons (SRD), **CM-pirate-zombie-captain** (use SRD zombie + captain NPC stats), rogue assassins.

### Ch 2 — Bullywug Gambit
- **Bullywug Temple under the City.** Bullywugs obsessed with the encroaching Savage Tide. Monsters: bullywugs (SRD), bullywug king (SRD), grippli scouts (use SRD grung stat block for grippli).

### Ch 6 — The Lightless Depths (Aboleth city)
- **Great Shaboath — Aboleth City Encounter.** Heroines uncover a slumbering city of aboleths and find the mysterious Star Gems. Monsters: aboleth (SRD), **CM-savant-aboleth** (custom CR-10 elder aboleth analog), skum (SRD) workers, shambling mound guardians.

### Ch 8 — Serpents of Scuttlecove
- **Sea Wyvern Approaches the Pirate City.** Slavers, cannibals, yuan-ti syndicate. Monsters: yuan-ti (SRD), sea hag (SRD), **CM-pirate-queen** (use SRD bandit captain + yuan-ti flavor).

### Ch 9–11 — Into the Maw / Wells of Darkness / Enemies of My Enemy
- **Demogorgon's Court.** Two-headed demon prince's hierarchy. Monsters: marilith (SRD), hezrou (SRD), nalfeshnee (SRD), glabrezu (SRD), ally succubus/incubus envoys.

### Ch 12 — Prince of Demons: Gaping Maw
- **Demogorgon, Prince of Demons.** Final BBEG confrontation. Two-headed demon lord. Monsters: **CM-demogorgon-prince-of-demons** (custom CR-26 demon lord stat block), demons of every type as an army encounter.
