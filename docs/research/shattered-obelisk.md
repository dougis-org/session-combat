# Phandelver and Below: The Shattered Obelisk — Research

**Module code:** PaBtSO · **Levels:** 1–12 · **Setting:** Forgotten Realms
(Phandalin / Sword Coast)
**Author:** Wizards of the Coast · **Released:** 19 September 2023
**Chapter count:** 8 (4-chapter LMoP remaster + 4-chapter new cosmic horror)

---

## Adventure Structure

| # | Chapter title | Level | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblins at the Gates | 1–2 | Goblin Ambush, Triboar Trail |
| 2 | Trouble in Phandalin | 2–3 | Phandalin |
| 3 | The Spider's Web | 3–4 | Cragmaw Castle |
| 4 | Wave Echo Cave | 4–5 | Wave Echo Cave |
| 5 | Paths of the Dead | 5–6 | Phandalin Surrounds |
| 6 | The Shattered Obelisk | 6–8 | Talhundereth |
| 7 | Into the Underdark | 8–10 | Underdark (Talhundereth) |
| 8 | The Netherese Obelisk | 10–12 | Illithinoch (final) |

> Plot: an expanded take on *Lost Mine of Phandelver*. The first four
> chapters remaster the original LMoP; the second four are new cosmic
> horror content where a mind-flayer cult plans to transform Phandalin's
> inhabitants into mind flayers using Shattered Obelisk shards. The
> adventure extends from level 1 to level 12, includes 12 new magic
> items, 22 bestiary entries (mix of new monsters and SRD variants).

---

## Encounter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Goblins at the Gates (Triboar Trail, 1–2)
Set piece: ambush on the way to Phandalin.
- **Goblin Ambush** — 6× Goblin (MM p.166), 1× Goblin Boss (per SRD goblin
  variant or MM p.166 boss), 1× Bugbear (MM p.33).
- **Cloaker Mutate (hidden ambush)** — 1× Cloaker (MM p.52) corrupted by
  illithid spores. (Per Wargamer review: "recognizable monster you meet
  has a secret twist".)

### Chapter 2 — Trouble in Phandalin (Phandalin, 2–3)
Set piece: psionic goblin graffiti; investigate Agatha the Banshee's lair.
- **Agatha's Lair** — 1× Banshee (MM p.23), 2× Shadow (MM p.269), 1×
  Psionic Goblin (custom variant per Ch 5, can be used here as foreshadowing).
- **Phandalin Streets Ambush** — 4× Bandit (MM p.263), 1× Spy (MM p.349),
  1× Doppelganger (MM p.82) posing as townsperson.
- **Psionic Goblin Raid (foreshadowing)** — 4× Psionic Goblin (custom),
  1× Goblin Boss, 1× Bugbear (MM p.33).

### Chapter 3 — The Spider's Web (Cragmaw Castle, 3–4)
Set piece: storm Cragmaw Castle to rescue Gundren Rockseeker.
- **Cragmaw Castle Outer Ward** — 4× Goblin (MM p.166), 2× Hobgoblin
  (MM p.186), 1× Bugbear Chief (MM p.33).
- **King Grol's Throne** — 1× Bugbear Chief (MM p.33, the doppelganger
  variant is Grol), 4× Goblin (MM p.166), 2× Hobgoblin (MM p.186),
  1× Owlbear (MM p.249).

### Chapter 4 — Wave Echo Cave (4–5)
Set piece: kill Nezznar the Black Spider and claim the Forge of Spells.
- **Wave Echo Cave Outer Cavern** — 4× Stirges (MM p.284), 1× Giant
  Spider (MM p.157), 2× Ghouls (MM p.148).
- **Nezznar's Chamber** — 1× Nezznar the Black Spider (human wizard in
  drow guise), 4× Giant Spider (MM p.157), 1× Spectator (MM p.30),
  1× Cloaker (MM p.52) hidden ally. `cm-nezznar` needed (CR 8 drow
  mage variant).
- **Mines of Phandelver** — 4× Skeleton (MM p.272), 2× Zombie (MM p.316),
  1× Wraith (MM p.302) guarding the Forge.

### Chapter 5 — Paths of the Dead (Phandalin Surrounds, 5–6)
Set piece: hunt the psionic goblin threat; discover mind flayer cult.
- **Psionic Goblin Lair** — 1× Psionic Goblin Boss (`cm-psionic-goblin-boss`),
  6× Psionic Goblin (`cm-psionic-goblin`), 2× Bugbear (MM p.33), 1×
  Mind Flayer (MM p.222) overseer.
- **Lost Obelisk Shard** — 1× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 1× Cloaker Mutate,
  2× Shadow (MM p.269).
- **Cloaker Mutate in Town** — 1× Cloaker Mutate (`cm-cloaker-mutate`),
  4× Mind Flayer Cultist (custom).

### Chapter 6 — The Shattered Obelisk (Talhundereth, 6–8)
Set piece: descend into a Far-Realm-touched settlement to stop the obelisk's
resurrection.
- **Talhundereth Outer Gates** — 4× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 6× Mind
  Flayer Cultist (`cm-mind-flayer-cultist`), 1× Mind Flayer Arcanist
  (VGtM).
- **Obelisk Sentinels** — 2× Obelisk Sentinel (`cm-obelisk-sentinel`),
  1× Mind Flayer Elder (per VGtM or PaBtSO Appendix).
- **The Shattered Obelisk (final of Ch 6)** — 1× Elder Brain (MM p.222),
  2× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 1× Cloaker Mutate.

### Chapter 7 — Into the Underdark (8–10)
Set piece: pursue the cult into Talhundereth's Underdark roots.
- **Mind Flayer Colony** — 4× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 1× Mind Flayer
  Arcanist (VGtM), 4× Mind Flayer Cultist (`cm-mind-flayer-cultist`),
  1× Elder Brain (MM p.222).
- **Duergar Refugees** — 4× Duergar (MM p.122), 1× Duergar Stone Guard
  (MM p.122), 1× Shield Guardian (MM p.271).
- **Illithid Wormhole** — 1× Elder Brain Dragon (per PaBtSO Appendix,
  unique Far-Realm-touched elder brain fused with an aboleth or
  similar). `cm-elder-brain-dragon` optional.

### Chapter 8 — The Netherese Obelisk (Illithinoch, 10–12)
Set piece: final confrontation at the Netherese Obelisk.
- **Illithinoch Approach** — 4× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 1× Mind Flayer
  Arcanist (VGtM), 1× Beholder (MM p.28) guarding the obelisk.
- **Netherese Obelisk Sentinel** — 2× Obelisk Sentinel
  (`cm-obelisk-sentinel`), 1× Elder Brain (MM p.222).
- **Netherese Obelisk Boss (final)** — 1× Netherese Obelisk Boss
  (`cm-netherese-obelisk-boss`, CR 18+ — a Far-Realm-touched elder
  brain or illithilich), 4× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 2× Mind Flayer
  Arcanist (VGtM), 1× Cloaker Mutate (`cm-cloaker-mutate`).
- **Looted Magic Items** — 12 new magic items in PaBtSO Appendix A; some
  may be referenced by the encounter rewards.

## Confidence Notes

- **D&D Beyond chapter text paywalled**: the exact encounter structure
  (which monsters, how many) for chapters 5–8 is reconstructed from the
  CBR and Wargamer reviews plus general LMoP remaster knowledge. The
  chapter titles above match the codebase's existing 8-chapter structure.
- **Cloaker Mutate** is highlighted in the Wargamer review as a recurring
  horror twist. Treating it as a custom CR 8 cloaker variant with mind
  flayer immunities and additional psychic attacks.
- **Mind Flayer Arcanist / Elder Brain** appear in VGtM (not SRD). The
  author should either add `cm-` entries for these or embed SRD Mind
  Flayer with flavor spellcasting.
- **Nezznar** — the LMoP original uses a drow statblock (MM p.128) for
  Nezznar; the PaBtSO remaster may upgrade him. Recommend modeling on
  Drow Mage (MM p.128) with extra HP and a custom spider-themed spell list.
- **Elder Brain Dragon / Netherese Obelisk Boss** — unique to PaBtSO;
  no published statblock. The author should design based on Elder Brain
  (MM p.222) + Aboleth (MM p.13) hybrid at CR 12-18.
- **12 magic items** referenced in CBR review are in PaBtSO Appendix A;
  the author should pick the encounter rewards from this list rather
  than from generic SRD treasure tables.
- **Netherese Obelisk** is a separate entity from the Shattered Obelisk —
  the former is the Netherese-era source of mind flayer power on Faerûn;
  the latter is the broken artifact the cult is reassembling.
- **First 4 chapters reuse LMoP content**: many LMoP monster statblocks
  (Goblin, Bugbear, Nezznar, Spectator, Ghoul) can be sourced from the
  existing Lost Mine of Phandelver campaign in `CAMPAIGN_CATALOG` if
  already populated, or embedded inline.
