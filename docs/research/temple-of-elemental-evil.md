# The Temple of Elemental Evil — Research

## Adventure structure

Classic T1–4 supermodule (1985), running levels 1–8 in the World of Greyhawk (village of Hommlet). Originally T1 *The Village of Hommlet* (1979) was a stand-alone introductory scenario; T2-T4 became the extended version. Written by Gary Gygax with Frank Mentzer, completing Gygax's original notes. Ranked 4th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004).

For G5 we treat T1-4 as a 6-chapter sampling covering the major campaign phases (village → moathouse → Nulb → temple outer → temple inner → Zuggtmoy):

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hommlet Village & Moathouse | 1–3 | Village of Hommlet / Moathouse | T1 content |
| 2 | Temple Outer Encounters | 3–5 | Temple of Elemental Evil (above ground) | T2 |
| 3 | Temple Level 1 | 3–5 | Underground Level 1 | T2 |
| 4 | Temple Level 2 | 4–6 | Underground Level 2 (water node) | T2 |
| 5 | Temple Level 3 | 5–8 | Underground Level 3 (fire node) | T3 |
| 6 | Temple Level 4 (Zuggtmoy) | 6–8 | Underground Level 4 (air node + demoness) | T4 finale |

## Encounter plan (6-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — Hommlet Village & Moathouse
- **Moathouse Reconnaissance.** Agents of the Hidden Shrine meet secretly at the ruined moathouse near Hommlet. Monsters: bandits (SRD), goblin sentries (SRD), a bugbear assassin.

### Ch 2 — Temple Outer Encounters
- **Patrol of the Temple Approach.** Hill giants, gnolls, and other humanoid raiders scout the perimeter. Monsters: gnoll (SRD), gnoll pack lord (SRD), **CM-temple-of-elemental-evil-priest** (human cleric of Elemental Evil).

### Ch 3 — Temple Level 1 (Earth Node)
- **Earth Temple Crypts.** Bandits, ogres, skeletons of past victims, and the hidden entrance to the elemental nodes. Monsters: ogre (SRD), skeletons (SRD), bugbear chief (SRD).

### Ch 4 — Temple Level 2 (Water Node)
- **Elemental Node of Water.** Water cultists, lizardfolk, gnolls, and lesser water elemental spawn. Monsters: lizardfolk (SRD), water cultists (SRD cultist), **CM-water-cultist-acolyte** (custom), grick (SRD).

### Ch 5 — Temple Level 3 (Fire Node)
- **Elemental Node of Fire.** Fire giants, salamanders, azer, magmin, fire cultists. Monsters: fire giant (SRD), magmin (SRD), salamander (SRD), **CM-fire-cultist-acolyte** (custom).

### Ch 6 — Temple Level 4 (Air Node & Zuggtmoy)
- **Zuggtmoy, Demoness Queen of Fungi.** Final BBEG. Air cultists, aerial servants, myconids (forced into service). Monsters: **CM-zuggtmoy** (custom CR-16 demoness), air cultists, myconid sovereign (use SRD myconid adult), **CM-air-cultist-acolyte** (custom), giant mantis-folk (use SRD grick).
