# Scarlet Citadel — Research

## Adventure structure

Third-party 5e mega-dungeon by Kobold Press (creator of Tales of the Valiant / Black Flag D&D-adjacent system). Originally part of the Tales of the Valiant line, also marketed for 5e. Levels 1–14. The Scarlet Citadel is a massive stronghold of mountain giants, ruled by the **Twilight Princess**, a stone giant mage NPC. Designed as a standalone mega-dungeon for parties of any level.

For G5 we treat it as an **8-chapter mega-dungeon sampling** (each major area of the citadel as a chapter):

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Approach & Outer Bailey | 1–3 | Mountain trail + outer bailey |
| 2 | The Garrison | 3–5 | Giant barracks |
| 3 | The Lower Citadel | 5–7 | Slave quarters + temple |
| 4 | The Twisting Halls | 5–8 | Labyrinthine mid-level |
| 5 | The Great Hall | 7–10 | Throne room area |
| 6 | The Twilight Tower | 10–12 | Mage tower |
| 7 | The Heart Vault | 12–14 | Treasure vault |
| 8 | The Twilight Princess | 13–14 | BBEG lair |

Final villain: **The Twilight Princess**, a stone giant evoker archmage. Allies include frost giant berserkers, ogres, and assorted elementals.

## Encounter plan (sample mega-dungeon encounters)

### Ch 1 — Approach & Outer Bailey
- **Hill Giant Patrol.** Hill giants on the mountain approach. Monsters: hill giant (SRD), **CM-stone-giant-pickets** (SRD stone giant), ogre (SRD).

### Ch 4 — Twisting Halls
- **Hill Giant Trapped Hallway.** The labyrinth shifts; giants and their goblinoid servants lurk. Monsters: hill giant (SRD), **CM-fire-giant-wrathguard** (SRD fire giant), goblin (SRD).

### Ch 5 — The Great Hall
- **Stone Giant Court.** The court of stone giants, with frost giant honor guards. Monsters: stone giant (SRD), frost giant (SRD), **CM-evoker-apprentice** (custom CR-6 stone giant mage).

### Ch 8 — The Twilight Princess
- **Twilight Princess, Stone Giant Evoker.** Final boss. Monsters: **CM-twilight-princess` (custom CR-14 stone giant evoker archmage)**, frost giant champion (custom CR-9), **CM-twilight-apostles` (custom CR-5 stone giant mages)**, air elemental (SRD).
