# Desert of Desolation — Research

## Adventure structure

I3–5 compilation (1987) of three classic Egyptian-themed modules (1982–1983) by Tracy Hickman (and Philip Meyers on I4). 1e AD&D. Levels 5–10 in the compilation, though original I3 was 5–7, I4 was 6–8, and I5 was 7–9. Set in a fictional Arabian-Nights-inspired desert. Compilation refitted for Forgotten Realms. Ranked 6th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004). The Hickmans' pre-Dragonlance work.

| Ch | Title | Author | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharaoh (I3) | Hickman | 5–7 | Pyramid of Amun-Re |
| 2 | Oasis of the White Palm (I4) | Hickman/Meyers | 6–8 | Oasis / Temple of Set / Crypt of Badr al-Mosak |
| 3 | Lost Tomb of Martek (I5) | Hickman | 7–9 | Glass sea / Crystal Prism / Mobius Tower / Citadel of Martek |

## Encounter plan (3-chapter mega-dungeon sampling)

### Ch 1 — Pharaoh: Pyramid of Amun-Re
- **Tomb of the Ghost Pharaoh.** Maze-filled pyramid with trap-laden corridors and the ghostly pharaoh Amun-Re. Monsters: **CM-amun-re-ghost-pharaoh** (custom CR-10 ghostly undead pharaoh), giant scorpions (SRD), mummy (SRD).

### Ch 2 — Oasis of the White Palm
- **Temple of Set & Crypt of Badr al-Mosak.** Kidnapped bride; cult of Set worshipping snake-god; lycanthropic jackalweres in the crypt. Monsters: jackalwere (SRD), **CM-set-priest** (custom CR-8 cleric of Set), giant constrictor snake (SRD).

### Ch 3 — Lost Tomb of Martek
- **Sea of Glass to the Crystal Prism.** Skate-ships across a glass sea, traverse a Crystal Prism to Mobius Tower, finally reach the Citadel of Martek. Monsters: **CM-martek-archmage** (custom CR-15 lich-wizard), **CM-guilder-failed-paladin** (CR-4 dimwitted paladin mutant), **CM-maddog-failed-rogue** (CR-4), efreet (SRD).
