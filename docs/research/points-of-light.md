# Points of Light — Research

## Adventure structure

4e D&D **Points of Light** campaign setting / mini-adventure series (2011, Neverwinter Campaign Setting). Levels 1–30 across 4 tiers (Heroic 1–10, Paragon 11–20, Epic 21–30). Set in the **Nentir Vale** region (4e default starting region). The campaign is structured around a series of "point of light" settlements under threat from the surrounding wilderness (classic 4e design philosophy). Each tier (heroic/paragon/epic) has associated adventure paths.

For G5 we treat the **Heroic tier** (3-chapter mini-path) as the starting campaign content:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starting Adventure (varies by guide) | 1–2 | One of the Nentir Vale points of light (Fallcrest, Winterhaven, etc.) |
| 2 | Heroic Adventure Path | 3–5 | Wilds surrounding the chosen starting town |
| 3 | Heroic Adventure Path Climax | 6–10 | Major threat (4e iconic "intrusion" events) |

The 4e **Points of Light** was the default campaign-setting presentation for D&D from 2008-2011. Core modules for the campaign series include:

- **H1: Keep on the Shadowfell** (4e conversion of B2 *The Keep on the Borderlands* — also in G5 list)
- **H2: Thunderspire Labyrinth** (4e)
- **H3: Assault on Nightwyrm Fortress** (4e)
- **P1: King of the Trollhaunt Warrens** (4e)
- **P2: Demon Queen's Enclave** (4e)
- **P3: Assault on Nightwyrm Fortress** (4e)

For the **3-chapter Points of Light** sampling in G5, we focus on a generic 3-step 4e starter path:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fallcrest Defense / Winterhaven Defense | 1–3 | Point of Light town |
| 2 | The Threatening Wilds | 3–5 | Wilderness intrusion |
| 3 | The Intruder Lair | 5–10 | Major villain's lair |

## Encounter plan (3-chapter sampling using SRD analogs)

### Ch 1 — Fallcrest Defense
- **Goblin Ambush on the King's Road.** Standard 4e starting encounter. Monsters: goblin (SRD), **CM-goblin-archer` (custom CR-1/4)**, dire boar (SRD).

### Ch 2 — The Threatening Wilds
- **Bandit Warlord's Raiders.** Mixed-bandit patrol; potentially a hobgoblin warlord or bandit captain. Monsters: bandit (SRD), **CM-bandit-warlord` (custom CR-4)**, hobgoblin (SRD).

### Ch 3 — The Intruder Lair
- **Boss: 4e Iconic Encounter.** Final fight against a 4e iconic monster. Monsters: **CM-bandit-king** (custom CR-8), hobgoblin captain (SRD), **CM-bandit-archmage` (custom CR-5 mage NPC)**, ogre (SRD).

## Confidence notes

- **Chapter structure**: LOW confidence. "Points of Light" was more a 4e design philosophy than a single adventure; the G5 entry likely refers to the 4e starter-set mini-campaigns.
- **Encounter structure**: LOW confidence. Body text is paywalled in 4e products; encounter details inferred from general 4e starter-set structure.
- **Monsters**: HIGH confidence on goblin/bandit/hobgoblin being in SRD; bandit-specific named NPCs need custom stat blocks.
- **Status**: This is 4e content; conversion to 5e requires significant custom work. DM will likely want to use *Essentials* conversions or build a custom 4e→5e conversion.
