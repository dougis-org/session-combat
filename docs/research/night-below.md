# Night Below — Research

## Adventure structure

3-book boxed set (1995), written by Carl Sargent, for 2nd Edition AD&D. Levels 1–14. Begins in the village of Haranshire and descends into the Underdark, culminating in the aboleth city of Great Shaboath on the Sunless Sea. Universally considered one of the best Underdark campaigns ever published. Cliff Ramshaw rated it 9/10 in *Arcane* magazine.

| Book | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Evils of Haranshire | 1–7 | Surface (Haranshire villages, magical flooding) |
| 2 | Perils of the Underdark | 8–12 | Underdark (derro, svirfneblin, illithids, rockseer elves) |
| 3 | The Sunless Sea | 11–14 | Aboleth city of Great Shaboath |

## Encounter plan (sample set-pieces per book)

### Book 1 — The Evils of Haranshire
- **Druid-Green Dragon Ambush in the Gnarley Forest.** Local druid ally turns out to be the false ally; reveals a young green dragon is orchestrating kidnappings. Monsters: **CM-gnarley-forest-druid** (custom CR-6 druid villain), young green dragon (SRD), bandits (SRD).
- **Slaving Ring in Haranshire Outskirts.** Small bands of brigands attempt to capture party spellcasters for the Underdark slave trade. Monsters: **CM-slaving-bandit** (custom, hidden arrow-attackers), goblin (SRD).

### Book 2 — Perils of the Underdark
- **Derro Caravan Encounter.** Dervish-like mad derro slave-catchers on the road; alliances with mind flayers. Monsters: derro (SRD analog: use SRD deep gnome stats with madness trait), **CM-derro-slaver-leader** (CR-5 derro warrior), mind flayer (SRD).
- **Rockseer Elf Clan Diplomacy.** A small colony of reclusive subterranean elves — potential allies. Monsters: **CM-rockseer-elf** (custom CR-3 elf ranger), goblin (SRD) scouts.

### Book 3 — The Sunless Sea
- **The Aboleth City of Great Shaboath.** Ancient aboleth civilization beneath the dark sea, with shambling mounds and ixitxachitl mind-slaves. Monsters: aboleth (SRD), **CM-savant-aboleth** (custom CR-10 elder aboleth), **CM-ixzan-ixitxachitl** (CR-4 vampire-squid demon), shambling mound (SRD), **CM-shaboath-golem** (CR-8 obsidian-flayed golem).
