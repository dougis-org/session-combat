# Journeys Through the Radiant Citadel — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `JTRC`
**Type:** Anthology (13 standalone adventures, written entirely by authors of color)
**Source:** WotC 5e, July 2022
**Levels:** 1–14
**Setting:** The Radiant Citadel (Ethreal Plane hub) — each adventure visits a
different home setting of a Citadel founder culture.

## Adventure structure (13 chapters)

Each chapter keys off a different civilization's home region reachable via the
Citadel. Levels scale 1 → 14. Locations are gazetteered in the book itself.

| # | Chapter (working title) | Level | Region / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Salted Legacy | 1 | Coastal village haunted by drowned dead; Igdir-inspired. |
| 2 | Written in Blood | 2 | Crimson Peak-inspired heist of a noble's tower. |
| 3 | The Fiend of Hollow Mine | 3 | Mexican *Día de Muertos* analogue — revolutionaries vs. fiend. |
| 4 | Wages of Vice | 4 | Fantasy Venice heist in a magiocracy. |
| 5 | Sins of the Elders | 5 | Caribbean-flavoured plague & elder aboleth. |
| 6 | Gold for Fools and Princes | 6 | Heist on a dragon-worshipping merchant cartel. |
| 7 | Trail of Destruction | 7 | Desert caravan escort against elemental fire cult. |
| 8 | In the Mists of Manivarsha | 8 | Indian-flavoured mist-haunted river pilgrimage. |
| 9 | Between Tangled Roots | 9 | Wood-elf druidic conclave defending a sacred tree. |
| 10 | Shadow of the Sun | 11 | Ethiopian-flavoured high-level political intrigue; magic carpets. |
| 11 | The Nightsea's Succor | 12 | Undersea merfolk kingdom diplomacy vs. aboleth tyrant. |
| 12 | Buried Dynasty | 13 | Underground tomb complex (Chinese-historical flavour). |
| 13 | Orchids of the Invisible Mountain | 14 | Final ascent to a hidden monastery; cosmic horror. |

(Note: chapter titles and orderings are reconstructed from Wargamer and BoLS
reviews plus D&D Beyond's product index. Levels are confirmed for the book
overall but per-chapter levels above are best estimates from review highlights.)

## Encounter plan

One standout encounter per chapter. Like Candlekeep, most monsters are SRD; new
custom monsters are flagged only where they are unique to the chapter.

- **1 · Salted Legacy** — *Drowned Town Skirmish.* **Drowned** (SRD *zombie* +
  water template) mob + a single **sea hag** (SRD) coordinating.
- **2 · Written in Blood** — *Noble's Tower Ambush.* **Cultists** + **mage**
  (SRD) plus a *pePPER-EATING competition* (social-only).
- **3 · The Fiend of Hollow Mine** — *Revolutionaries vs. Fiend.* **Drow**
  patrol, **gnoll pack** (SRD), and the **Fiend of Hollow Mine** (new monster).
- **4 · Wages of Vice** — *Magical Heist.* **Succubus/incubus** + **doppelganger**
  bodyguards (SRD).
- **5 · Sins of the Elders** — *Elder Aboleth Reef.* **Sahuagin** + **merfolk** +
  an **aboleth** (SRD).
- **6 · Gold for Fools and Princes** — *Cartel Heist.* **Half-red dragon veteran**
  (SRD templates) and **magmin** crews.
- **7 · Trail of Destruction** — *Fire Cult Caravan.* **Fire giant** + **magmin**
  + **azer** patrol (SRD).
- **8 · In the Mists of Manivarsha** — *River Pilgrimage.* **Nagpa** + **harginn**
  swarms (SRD *ettercap* + flavour).
- **9 · Between Tangled Roots** — *Druidic Conclave Defense.* **Druids** +
  **awakened trees** vs. **fomorian** siege (SRD).
- **10 · Shadow of the Sun** — *High-Level Political Heist.* **Vampire spellcaster**
  + **assassin** retinue (SRD).
- **11 · The Nightsea's Succor** — *Merfolk Court & Aboleth Tyrant.* **Aboleth**
  + **merrow** + **sea spawn** (SRD).
- **12 · Buried Dynasty** — *Underground Tomb.* **Yuan-ti malisons** + **bone
  naga** + **ghost** swarms (SRD).
- **13 · Orchids of the Invisible Mountain** — *Cosmic Monastery Ascent.*
  **Githyanki knight** + **mind flayer arcanist** + an **eye of the mountain**
  (new monster — cosmic horror final boss).

## Confidence notes

- The chapter **titles** above are reconstructed from Wargamer review callouts
  and BoLS spotlights — full chapter titles are paywalled on D&D Beyond. The
  exact ordering and per-chapter level assignment are best-effort; the author
  agent should verify against the table of contents before locking encounters.
- The book's level range (1–14) and the "no overarching campaign" framing are
  confirmed across all three review sources.
- Many chapters are social / exploration-heavy and can be lightly adapted to a
  single encounter if the author agent wants to keep the encounter count
  manageable for a 13-chapter file (vs. one encounter per chapter as listed).
