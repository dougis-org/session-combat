# Queen of the Spiders — Research

## Adventure structure

GDQ1–7 supermodule (1986), combining seven classic 1e AD&D modules into one integrated 9-level campaign arc: G1 Steading of the Hill Giant Chief → G2 Glacial Rift of the Frost Giant Jarl → G3 Hall of the Fire Giant King → D1 Descent into the Depths of the Earth → D2 Shrine of the Kuo-Toa → D3 Vault of the Drow → Q1 Queen of the Demonweb Pits. Levels 8–14 nominally; rewards extended play. Set in Greyhawk. Voted #1 greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004).

For G5 research purposes, treat this as a 4-chapter sampling (mega-dungeon pattern per the rollout doc instructions).

## Encounter plan (4-chapter mega-dungeon sampling)

### Ch 1 (G1/G2 sample) — The Hill & Frost Giant Alliance
- **Steading of the Hill Giant Chief.** Initial foray into giant-allied steading; ogre mercenaries, hill giant guards, hints of letters from "drow." Monsters: hill giant (SRD), ogre (SRD), goblins (SRD).

### Ch 2 (G3/D1 sample) — Fire Giant King & Underdark Entry
- **Hall of King Snurre.** Fire giant stronghold, hell hound patrols, secret passage leading deep into the Underdark. Monsters: fire giant (SRD), hell hound (SRD), drow patrol ambush (use SRD drow + **CM-drow-patrol** as a themed encounter).

### Ch 3 (D2/D3 sample) — Kuo-Toa & Vault of the Drow
- **Erelhei-Cinlu (drow city) Intrigue.** First major drow encounter: patrol, priestess Eclavdra, temple of Lolth. Monsters: drow (SRD), drider (SRD), **CM-eclavdra-priestess** (CR-13 drow priestess, custom), quaggoth slaves.

### Ch 4 (Q1 sample) — Queen of the Demonweb Pits
- **Lolth, Demon Queen of Spiders.** Astral gate to the Abyss and Lolth's layer. Monsters: **CM-lolth-demon-queen** (CR-26+ demon lord), drider guards (SRD), **CM-drow-priestess-of-Lolth** (high-CR wizard/cleric combo), giant spider swarms (SRD), phase spiders (SRD).
