# The Shackled City Adventure Path — Research

## Adventure structure

12-chapter *Dungeon* magazine Adventure Path (March 2003 – November 2004, plus a 13th chapter added in the 2007 hardcover collection), running levels 1–20. Set in the city of Cauldron, nominally in Greyhawk but easily reskinnable. Won the 2006 ENnie gold for "Best Adventure" and "Best Campaign Setting/Supplement."

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Life's Bazaar
- **Skum-Prowled Sewer Ambush.** Party tracks missing children into Cauldron's flooded underdark beneath the city. Monsters: skum (SRD), grimlocks (SRD), derro sneak team (use SRD derro).

### Ch 2 — Drakthar's Way (vampiric bugbear)
- **Drakthar's Goblin Warren.** A vampiric bugbear leads goblin raids from a cave complex. Monsters: goblins (SRD), bugbear chief (SRD), **CM-drakthar-vampire-bugbear** (custom, CR ~5). Use existing SRD bugbear as base + vampire template.

### Ch 7 — Secrets of the Soul Pillars
- **Wee Jas Temple Assault.** Templars of the death goddess Wee Jas — undead assassins sent against the party. Monsters: **CM-shackled-city-assassin** (custom NPC: temple-trained shadowdancer), Clay Golem (SRD), skull-themed lich-priest.

### Ch 8 — Lords of Oblivion
- **Beholder Overlord's Lair Beneath Cauldron.** Eye tyrant conducts unholy rituals beneath the city. Monsters: beholder (SRD), beholder zombie thralls.

### Ch 9–10 — Foundation of Flame & Thirteen Cages
- **Cagewright Volcano Siege.** Demodands (specifically, kelubar / shator-demodands) pour through a planar rift as Cauldron's volcano erupts. Monsters: shator demodand (SRD analog: use armanite or star spawn), fire elementals (SRD), a young red dragon awakens in the caldera.

### Ch 11 — Strike on Shatterhorn
- **Jungle Hunt on Shatterhorn.** Final Cagewright remnants tracked to an ancient yuan-ti temple. Monsters: yuan-ti (SRD), giant constrictor snakes (SRD), yuan-ti abomination boss.

### Ch 12 — Asylum
- **Adimarchus, Demon Prince of Madness.** The imprisoned Demon Prince waits in his skullrot asylum. Monsters: **CM-adimarchus-demon-prince** (custom CR-22+ demon lord stat block), manes/lemures horde (SRD), chain-wrapped bone devil lieutenant.
