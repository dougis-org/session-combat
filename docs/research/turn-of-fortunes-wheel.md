# Planescape: Turn of Fortune's Wheel — Research

## Adventure structure

5e hardcover adventure (2022, included in *Keys from the Golden Vault*) written by Justice Ramin Arman. Originally published as part of the *Keys from the Golden Vault* anthology but is a 14-chapter planar mega-dungeon spanning the Great Wheel: Sigil, the Outlands, and the Outer Planes (Mechanus, Arborea, Elysium, the Beastlands, Gehenna, the Nine Hells, Gehenna, the Abyss, etc.).

The campaign centers around the **tulpa War**: a war between two factions — the **Heroes of Sigil** (reformed adventures) and the **Tulpa Raiders** (rogue tulpas bent on ontological revolution). The heroes are recruited to recover an artifact called the **Spindle of Sinbad** from the Vault of the Planes. The campaign uses the planar structure of Planescape to traverse Sigil's city and the Outer Planes.

For G5 we treat it as a **14-chapter adventure path** spanning the planes:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hirelings & Heroes | 1–3 | Sigil (introduction) |
| 2 | Keys to the Vault | 3–5 | Vault of the Planes entrance |
| 3 | Sigil Patrol | 4–6 | Planar intrigue in Sigil |
| 4–14 | Each chapter on a different Outer Plane | 6–14 | One plane per chapter (Mechanus, Arborea, Elysium, Beastlands, Gehenna, Nine Hells, etc.) |

The campaign is the longest "Planescape" themed 5e adventure and the only one to literally span all 17 Outer Planes (each chapter sampling one).

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Hirelings & Heroes (Sigil)
- **Sigil Street Patrol.** The party is recruited by the Heroes of Sigil. Monsters: **CM-signers** (SRD guard analog), **CM-tulpa-reformer-rogue** (custom CR-2 rogue).

### Ch 4 — Mechanus (Plane of Order)
- **Modron Cube Patrol.** Modrons encounter the party as planar intruders. Monsters: modron (SRD), **CM-modron-ordinator** (custom CR-4, use SRD modron stats).

### Ch 7 — The Beastlands
- **Beastlands Hunt.** Powerful beast creatures (good-aligned). Monsters: **CM-beast-lord-guardian** (custom CR-8), giant eagle (SRD).

### Ch 9 — The Nine Hells (Baator)
- **Dispater's Fortress.** Archdevil intrigue in Dis. Monsters: **CM-dispater-archdevil` (custom CR-21)**, **CM-pit-fiend-lieutenant` (SRD pit fiend)**, **CM-devil-courier` (custom CR-3 imp messenger)**.

### Ch 12 — The Abyss (Demon Lords)
- **Demon Lord Encounter.** A demon lord's layer. Monsters: **CM-balor-sergeant` (custom CR-12)**, hezrou (SRD), marilith (SRD).

### Ch 14 — Final Vault
- **The Spindle of Sinbad.** The artifact reveals an ancient evil. Monsters: **CM-vault-sentinel` (custom CR-15 construct)**, **CM-tulpa-puppet-master` (custom CR-12 rogue)**, **CM-planar-constructs` (custom CR-6 golem)**.
