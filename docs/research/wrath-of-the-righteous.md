# Wrath of the Righteous (Pathfinder Adventure Path) — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `WotR`
**Type:** Adventure Path (Pathfinder 1e, 2013; mythic tier)
**Source:** Paizo, August 2013 – February 2014
**Levels:** 1 → ~20 (with Mythic paths)
**Setting:** Worldwound, Mendev (Golarion) — Crusade against the demon lord
Deskari. Includes the **Mythic Adventures** rules subsystem.

## Adventure structure (6 chapters)

| # | Chapter | Level | Author | Location / hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Worldwound Incursion | 1–4 | Amber E. Scott | Kenabres, Mendev — demons assault the crusader city. |
| 2 | Sword of Valor | 5–8 | Neil Spicer | Drezen — recapture the citadel and the Banner of Valor. |
| 3 | Demon's Heresy | 9–11 | Jim Groves | Worldwound — strike the Templars of the Ivory Labyrinth. |
| 4 | The Midnight Isles | 12–13 | James Jacobs & Greg A. Vaughan | Midnight Isles, Abyss — Nocticula's realm and the mythic crystal source. |
| 5 | Herald of the Ivory Labyrinth | 14–16 | Wolfgang Baur | Ivory Labyrinth, Abyss — rescue Iomedae's herald from Baphomet. |
| 6 | City of Locusts | 17–20 | Richard Pett | Iz, Abyss — final confrontation with Deskari. |

## Encounter plan

Two to three encounters per chapter. Mythic-tier encounters mix standard
demons with new Mythic-style elites. Pathfinder demons map to 5e SRD with
occasional new stat blocks needed for the mythic antagonists.

### Chapter 1 — The Worldwound Incursion

- **Demonic Incursion at Kenabres.** **Dretch** + **babau** (SRD) led by the
  cultist **Staunton Vhane** (new monster — fallen paladin cultist, CR 5).
- **Minagho's Ambush.** The **Balor** Minagho (SRD *balor* + **glabrezu** template,
  CR 13) attacks the Wardstone.

### Chapter 2 — Sword of Valor

- **Drezen's Courtyard.** **Vrock** swarm + **babaus** (SRD) led by a **marilith**
  (SRD).
- **The Black Dragon Izile.** **Adult black dragon** (SRD) defending the citadel.
- **The Banner of Valor.** A **balor** named **Khorramzadeh** (new monster —
  *balor* + barbarian-tyrant, CR 14) holds the Banner.

### Chapter 3 — Demon's Heresy

- **Templars of the Ivory Labyrinth.** **Tainted crusader** mobs (new monster —
  paladin-template corrupted, CR 8 each).
- **Xanthir Vang (Underground Lair).** **Dretch**, **shadow demon**, **nabassu**
  (SRD), and **Xanthir Vang** (new monster — mythic lich-demon, CR 12).

### Chapter 4 — The Midnight Isles

- **Midnight Isles Port.** **Succubus** + **vrock** + **incubus** mobs (SRD).
- **Nocticula's Court (social + combat).** Nocticula herself (new monster —
  CR 20 succubus-queen-turned-elven-deity) with **gynosphinx** advisor.

### Chapter 5 — Herald of the Ivory Labyrinth

- **Heralds of Baphomet.** **Minotaur** + **glabrezu** (SRD) led by a **behir**.
- **The Herald's Prison.** **Pit fiend** + **bone devil** army (SRD) with the
  **Herald of Iomedae** as the rescue target.

### Chapter 6 — City of Locusts

- **The City of Locusts Approach.** **Balor** patrol + **pit fiend** vanguard.
- **Deskari's Throne.** **Deskari, Lord of the Locust Host** (new monster — CR
  22 demon lord with mythic swarm traits) with **balor** + **succubus** court.
- **Closing the Worldwound (finale).** Mythic-tier resolution; depending on
  the Mythic path the party chose, finale may be **Areelu Vorlesh** (new monster
  — CR 18 mythic-scientist half-demon) or a mythic path-specific variant.

## Confidence notes

- Wrath of the Righteous introduced the **Mythic Adventures** ruleset to
  Pathfinder 1e — a milestone mechanic that allowed players to take
  one of several *mythic paths* (Angel, Demon, Gold Dragon, Hellknight,
  Hierophant, Lich, Trickster). The 5e conversion would need a custom
  mythic subsystem or a simplified "epic destiny" mechanic.
- D&D Beyond chapter bodies are not applicable here — this is a Pathfinder
  AP. The most authoritative sources are PathfinderWiki and the Paizo
  product page.
- No official 5e conversion of WotR has been published by Paizo (as of
  August 2026). The author agent will need to port monsters from the
  Pathfinder Bestiary 6 (Mythic Adventures) and the AP PDFs.
- Stat blocks for the major bosses (Deskari, Areelu, Nocticula, Baphomet,
  Xanthir Vang, Khorramzadeh) are available in the AP PDFs; transcribe
  the Pathfinder stat blocks and convert using the Kingmaker Bestiary
  (5E) conversion ratios as reference.
