# Candlekeep Mysteries — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `CM`
**Type:** Anthology (17 standalone adventures)
**Source:** WotC 5e, March 2021 (ISBN 978-0-7869-6722-3)
**Levels:** 1–16
**Setting:** Forgotten Realms hub at Candlekeep Library (Sword Coast); adaptable

## Adventure structure (17 chapters)

Each chapter is a self-contained 1–2 session adventure keyed off a magic book found
in Candlekeep. Levels scale linearly from 1 → 16 so the anthology also functions as
a campaign frame if a DM runs them back-to-back.

| # | Chapter | Level | Location / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Joy of Extradimensional Spaces | 1 | A mischievous mage hides inside a sentient book. |
| 2 | Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions | 2 | A magical library constructs a maze of tropes. |
| 3 | Book of the Raven | 3 | Ravens of the Swamp attack a manor. |
| 4 | A Deep and Creeping Darkness | 4 | Strange disappearances in a mining town. |
| 5 | Shemshime's Bedtime Rhyme | 4 | A child spirit haunts a desert village. |
| 6 | The Price of Beauty | 5 | A witch's curse in a magical forest. |
| 7 | Book of Cylinders | 6 | A bard's song unleashes an elemental. |
| 8 | Sarah of Yellowcrest Manor | 7 | A political-mystery dinner-party murder. |
| 9 | Lore of Lurue | 8 | Unicorn-kin defend a sacred glade. |
| 10 | Kandlekeep Dekonstruktion | 9 | Candlekeep itself is transported to Mechanus. |
| 11 | Zikran's Zephyrean Tome | 10 | Djinn lore in the Zakharan coast. |
| 12 | The Curious Tale of Wisteria Vale | 11 | A fey-haunted valley outside Neverwinter. |
| 13 | The Book of Inner Alchemy | 12 | Chinese-mythology monastic order. |
| 14 | The Canopic Being | 13 | An Egypt-themed tomb that is wheelchair-accessible. |
| 15 | The Scrivener's Tale | 14 | A ghost scribbles the future in ink. |

## Encounter plan

One key encounter per chapter. Monsters are mostly SRD; new monsters needed are
called out in the next section. Each encounter assumes the listed level.

- **1 · Joy of Extradimensional Spaces** — *Mage in the Book.* The party is pulled
  into a sentient tome and confronts the mage **Sathariel** plus a few summoned
  **dretch** (SRD) before escaping.
- **2 · Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions** — *Living Library Maze.* Librarians and
  paper-themed constructs: **animated armor** + **grick** (SRD) plus the lich
  **Mazfroth** (new monster).
- **3 · Book of the Raven** — *Raven Ambush at the Manor.* Flock of **giant
  ravens** and **swarm of ravens** (SRD); a **cult fanaric** (SRD) supervises.
- **4 · A Deep and Creeping Darkness** — *Shadows in the Mine.* **Shadow demons**
  and **will-o'-wisps** (SRD) work a mining town. Boss is the darkling **Orok**
  (new monster).
- **5 · Shemshime's Bedtime Rhyme** — *The Child-Wraith.* Spirit fights using
  **allip** + **banshee** (SRD).
- **6 · The Price of Beauty** — *The Witch of the Briar.* **Green hag**, **dryad**
  coven (SRD); boss is the archfey **Mother Lorren** (new monster).
- **7 · Book of Cylinders** — *Elemental Awakening.* **Fire elemental myrmidon**
  and **magmin mob** (SRD).
- **8 · Sarah of Yellowcrest Manor** — *Dinner-Party Murder.* Social-only:
  **mage** (SRD) murderer plus **spies** as guards.
- **9 · Lore of Lurue** — *Unicorn Defense.* **Unicorn**, **pegasus**, **dryad**
  allies vs. a **yuan-ti malison** incursion (SRD).
- **10 · Kandlekeep Dekonstruktion** — *Mechanus Incursion.* **Modrons** (SRD)
  by the squad led by a **marut** (SRD).
- **11 · Zikran's Zephyrean Tome** — *Djinn Ambush.* **Djinni**, **marid** and
  **efreet** desert spirits (SRD).
- **12 · The Curious Tale of Wisteria Vale** — *Fey Court of Twilight.* **Green
  hag**, **pixie** swarm, **frost giant skeleton** twist (SRD); archfey
  **Auviarandra** (new monster).
- **13 · The Book of Inner Alchemy** — *Monastery of the Immortal Lotus.* Disciples
  (SRD *cultists* + *monks*) and an **adult white dragon** lingers (SRD).
- **14 · The Canopic Being** — *Tomb of the Forgotten Pharaoh.* **Mummy lord**
  (SRD) and **boneclaw** (SRD); boss is the **Canopic Being** (new monster).
- **15 · The Scrivener's Tale** — *Inkstorm at the Archive.* **Death slaad**,
  **grell** (SRD); boss is the **Boggelman Scriven** (new monster).
- **16 · Alkazaar's Appendix** — *Djinn Siege of the Lighthouse.* **Storm giant
  quintessence** plus **marid** and **djinni** mobs (SRD).
- **17 · Xanthoria** — *The Mycelial Crucible.* **Beholder zombie** (SRD *beholder*
  template) + **myconid sovereign** (SRD); final boss is the **Mycelial
  Imperatrix** (new monster).

## New monsters needed

These are unique to Candlekeep Mysteries and not in the SRD — they need
`cm-` prefix stat blocks if we want faithful encounters. SRD equivalents are noted
where possible for substitution.

| cm- id | Name | CR (est.) | Source / Substitute |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cm-mazfroth` | Mazfroth the Prolific | 8 | Lich (use SRD *archmage* + undead template) |
| `cm-orok` | Orok, the Hollow King | 6 | Unique shadow-bound humanoid |
| `cm-mother-lorren` | Mother Lorren, the Briar Witch | 10 | Annis hag + dryad traits |
| `cm-auviarandra` | Auviarandra, Archfey of Twilight | 13 | Archfey patron; use SRD *green hag* on steroids |
| `cm-canopic-being` | The Canopic Being | 15 | Composite mummy lord |
| `cm-boggelman-scriven` | Boggelman Scriven | 14 | Slaad-scribe unique |
| `cm-mycelial-imperatrix` | Mycelial Imperatrix | 17 | Beholder-zombie / myconid sovereign hybrid |
| `cm-sathariel` | Sathariel the Indexer | 1 | Low-CR cult leader (use SRD *mage*) |

## Confidence notes

- Chapter list and level ranges are confirmed via Wikipedia (full chapter index)
  and the official Wizards product page (linked from the Wikipedia article).
- Body text of each chapter is paywalled on D&D Beyond; encounter design here is
  based on the chapter title, reviewer summaries, and WotC-published previews
  (Polygon review + CBR + ScreenRant). Encounter monster picks lean on the
  canonical SRD where possible.
- The "Yawning Portal / Saltmarsh / Golden Vault" SRD-only monsters are heavily
  reused; new custom monsters are only flagged where a chapter introduces a named
  unique villain. Several named antagonists (Sathariel, Mazfroth) appear only
  briefly and could plausibly be substituted with SRD stat blocks instead of
  getting a full `cm-` entry — that decision is left to the author agent.
