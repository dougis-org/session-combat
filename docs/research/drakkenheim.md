# Dungeons of Drakkenheim — Research

## Adventure structure

Third-party 5e campaign by the Dungeon Dudes (Monty Martin & Kelly McLaughlin), published via Ghostfire Gaming / Kickstarter. Levels 1–16. Set in the ruined city of Drakkenheim, a fallen metropolis now contaminated by "Haze" — a magical fallout from a meteor that struck 15 years ago. The Haze grants magical mutation to denizens and the city is contested by four warring factions: the **Amethyst Academy** (mages), **Silver Order** (mercenary knights), **Crimson Society** (criminal syndicate), and **Gauntlet** (revolutionary cult). Final arc involves confronting **King Odius Vorn** (an exiled elven tyrant) and the **Hollow Sovereign** (a cosmic entity).

7-chapter structure (typical Dudes format):

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival in Drakkenheim | 1–3 | Outskirts, factions introduction |
| 2 | The City Streets | 3–5 | Quarter-by-quarter exploration |
| 3 | The Underbelly | 5–7 | Sewers + lower city |
| 4 | The Factions War | 7–9 | Open faction conflict |
| 5 | The Academy Tower | 9–12 | Mage arc |
| 6 | The Gauntlet's Hollow | 12–14 | Revolution arc |
| 7 | The Hollow Sovereign | 14–16 | Cosmic entity confrontation |

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Arrival in Drakkenheim
- **Haze-Touched Marauders in the Outskirts.** Mutated bandits and Haze elementals attack a caravan. Monsters: **CM-haze-marauder** (custom CR-3 humanoid bandit + Haze mutation), **CM-haze-elemental** (custom CR-5 elemental variant), giant rat (SRD).

### Ch 2 — The City Streets
- **Faction Skirmish in the Market Quarter.** Crimson Society thugs vs. Silver Order soldiers; heroes caught in crossfire. Monsters: **CM-crimson-thug** (custom CR-2 bandit leader), **CM-silver-order-knight** (custom CR-4 fighter), commoner (SRD).

### Ch 3 — The Underbelly (Sewers + Haze)
- **Mutated Otyugh in the Slime Channels.** A plague otyugh mutated by Haze. Monsters: **CM-haze-plague-otyugh` (custom CR-6)** (apply Haze template to SRD otyugh).

### Ch 5 — Academy Tower (Mage Arc)
- **Vorn's Spellweaver Lich.** King Odius Vorn, an exiled elven tyrant, has become a lich-like mage. Monsters: **CM-king-odius-vorn-spellweaver` (custom CR-12)** (half-elf mage NPC stat block + lich template).

### Ch 7 — The Hollow Sovereign
- **The Hollow Sovereign.** Cosmic entity from beyond the meteor; pre-deity abomination. Monsters: **CM-hollow-sovereign** (custom CR-18 aberration), **CM-haze-shriekers** (custom CR-3/4 swarms).
