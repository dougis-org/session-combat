# Reavers of Harkenwold — Research

## Adventure structure

4e D&D Starter Set adventure (2010), written by Bruce R. Cordell and Claudio Pozas. Levels 1–5 (4e heroic tier). Set in the **Harkenwold** region of the **Nentir Vale** setting. The Halfling province of Harkenwold is occupied by the Iron Circle (an organization of bogun-troll-led raiders). Final villain: **Twigsplitter**, an ettin chieftain in the Iron Circle army. Designed as an introductory D&D Encounters-style adventure.

For G5 we treat it as a **5-chapter adventure path**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Into Harkenwold | 1–2 | Village of Talwod (rescue mission) |
| 2 | The Iron Circle | 2–3 | Iron Circle patrols |
| 3 | The Doom Marsh | 3–4 | Marsh-based encounters |
| 4 | Ironfang Keep | 4–5 | Final fortress |
| 5 | Twigsplitter | 5 | BBEG ettin chieftain |

## Encounter plan (5-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — Into Harkenwold
- **Village Defense in Talwod.** Halfling townsfolk are under siege by Iron Circle. Monsters: **CM-iron-circle-conscript` (custom CR-1)** (use SRD guard stat block), **CM-bogun-reaver` (custom CR-2)** (use SRD hobgoblin).

### Ch 2 — The Iron Circle
- **Iron Circle Patrol Ambush.** Mixed force of bogun-trolls and hobgoblin officers. Monsters: **CM-iron-circle-officer` (custom CR-3)** (use SRD hobgoblin captain), **CM-bogun-troll** (custom CR-4) (use SRD bugbear + troll hybrid).

### Ch 3 — The Doom Marsh
- **Marsh Troll Lair.** A troll lair in the bog. Monsters: **CM-marsh-troll-chieftain** (custom CR-5 troll variant), **CM-bogun-shaman` (custom CR-2 shaman)**, giant frog (SRD).

### Ch 4 — Ironfang Keep
- **Ironfang Keep Assault.** Final fortress with ogre guards and a half-orc commander. Monsters: ogre (SRD), **CM-half-orc-iron-circle-commander` (custom CR-4)**, hobgoblin (SRD).

### Ch 5 — Twigsplitter
- **Twigsplitter, Ettin Chieftain.** Final BBEG. Monsters: **CM-twigsplitter-ettin-chieftain` (custom CR-6 ettin)**, ettin (SRD), hobgoblin lieutenants (SRD).

## Confidence notes

- **Chapter structure**: MEDIUM-HIGH confidence. The 4e Starter Set structure is well-documented in 4e reviews.
- **Encounter structure**: MEDIUM confidence. 4e stat blocks need conversion to 5e for use as custom monsters.
- **Monsters**: HIGH confidence on Iron Circle being a hobgoblin-based evil faction. Bogun-trolls are a 4e monster with no direct SRD analog.
- **Status**: 4e original; 5e adaptation required. DMs Guild has unofficial 5e conversions.
