# Empire of the Ghouls — Research

## Adventure structure

5e hardcover adventure (2022, included in *Journeys Through the Radiant Citadel* anthology) written by Dominique Dickey, with additional chapters by other authors. Originally a Planescape-themed anthology but *Empire of the Ghouls* is a separate 6-chapter campaign arc. Levels 5–10. Set on the **Radiant Citadel** (an Ethereal Plane hub city connected to 14 civilizations across the Material Plane) and eventually descending into the Ghoul Imperium — a layer of the **Abyss** ruled by **Doresain**, the Ghoul King.

The campaign is unusual: each chapter has a hub city (one of the 14 Radiant Citadel civilizations) plus a planar excursion to a different location. Final villain: **Doresain, the Ghoul King** in his Ghoul Imperium layer.

For G5 we treat it as a **6-chapter planar adventure**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival at the Radiant Citadel | 5–6 | Radiant Citadel |
| 2 | First Hub Civilization | 6–7 | Civilization A (TBD) |
| 3 | Second Hub Civilization | 7–8 | Civilization B (TBD) |
| 4 | The Descent | 8–9 | Ghoul Imperium (Abyss layer) |
| 5 | Doresain's Court | 9–10 | Ghoul King's throne |
| 6 | The Hidden Truth | 10 | Radiant Citadel finale |

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Radiant Citadel
- **Radiant Citadel Exploration.** The party explores the hub city and learns of the ghoul threat. Monsters: **CM-radiant-citadel-guard` (custom CR-3)**, **CM-merciful-construct** (custom CR-5), giant eagle (SRD).

### Ch 2 — First Hub Civilization
- **Ghoul-Cult Infiltration.** A ghoul cult is operating in one of the hub civilizations. Monsters: ghoul (SRD), **CM-ghoul-cultist** (custom CR-2), **CM-ghoul-master` (use SRD ghoul + cleric template)**.

### Ch 4 — The Descent into the Ghoul Imperium
- **Ghoul Imperium Onward March.** The party crosses into Doresain's layer. Monsters: ghoul (SRD), ghast (SRD), **CM-allip` (use SRD allip)**.

### Ch 5 — Doresain's Court
- **The Ghoul King's Throne Room.** Final boss. Monsters: **CM-doresain-ghoul-king` (custom CR-15 demon lord/ghoul archetype)**, ghast (SRD), **CM-ghoul-king-bodak` (custom CR-6)**, **CM-ghoul-demon-prince-minion` (custom CR-8)**.

### Ch 6 — The Hidden Truth (Radiant Citadel)
- **The 12 Missing Founders.** Reveals that the Radiant Citadel has a hidden threat. Monsters: **CM-hidden-founder-revenant` (custom CR-7 revenant)**, **CM-radiant-citadel-elder-council** (use SRD noble).
