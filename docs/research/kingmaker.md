# Kingmaker (Pathfinder Adventure Path) — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `KM`
**Type:** Adventure Path (Pathfinder 1e, 2010; 5e conversion via 2022 hardcover)
**Source:** Paizo, March 2010 – August 2010 (5e hardcover October 2022)
**Levels:** 1 → ~17
**Setting:** River Kingdoms, Golarion (Pathfinder setting) — kingdom-building
campaign.

## Adventure structure (6 chapters)

| # | Chapter | Level | Author | Location / hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stolen Land | 1–3 | Tim Hitchcock | Stolen Lands, Brevoy — overthrow the bandit Stag Lord. |
| 2 | Rivers Run Red | 4–5 | Robert G. McCreary | Stolen Lands — defend fledgling kingdom from monsters. |
| 3 | The Varnhold Vanishing | 6–7 | Greg A. Vaughan | Varnhold — investigate the missing colony. |
| 4 | Blood for Blood | 8–10 | Neil Spicer | Hooktongue Slough — barbarian incursion. |
| 5 | War of the River Kings | 11–13 | Jason Nelson | Pitax — tournament with political intrigue. |
| 6 | Sound of a Thousand Screams | 14–17 | Richard Pett | First World — Nyrissa's curse on the land. |

The 2022 hardcover (5e conversion) preserves the chapter structure but renumbers
the 5e content under one cover with a 5e-bestiary companion book.

## Encounter plan

Two to three encounters per chapter covering major set-pieces. As with RotR,
Pathfinder-specific monsters dominate and need custom stat blocks.

### Chapter 1 — Stolen Land

- **The Stag Lord's Fort.** **Bandits** (SRD), **bugbear** captains (SRD), and
  **Auchs the Stag Lord** (new monster — half-elf barbarian bandit-king, CR 6).
- **Tuskwater Ogre Lair.** **Ogre** + **ogre brute** (SRD) led by the **Tuskwater
  Ogre Chief** (new monster — CR 5).
- **Kobold Lair.** **Kobolds** (SRD) — lowest-level encounter for the AP.

### Chapter 2 — Rivers Run Red

- **Owlcat Menace.** **Owlbear** (SRD) + **owlbear terror** variant (new
  monster — CR 4).
- **Troll Lair.** **Trolls** (SRD) led by **Kharzum the Troll-Lord** (new monster
  — barbarian troll, CR 9).
- **Sootscale Kobolds.** **Kobolds** (SRD) led by a **sorcerer kobold** (new
  monster — CR 3).

### Chapter 3 — The Varnhold Vanishing

- **Varnhold Village.** Social/roleplay; no major combat.
- **The Tomb of Varn.** **Skeletons** + **wraiths** (SRD), then **dryads** +
  **sirines** (SRD) guarding the cave.
- **The Fungal Bloom.** **Myconid adults** + **shambling mound** (SRD) with the
  **Bloom Infused Druid** (new monster — CR 7, plant-themed).

### Chapter 4 — Blood for Blood

- **Barbarian Raid on the Capital.** **Berserkers** + **half-orc warriors**
  (SRD) led by the **Sovnya Clan Chief** (new monster — CR 9).
- **The Slough Lizardfolk.** **Lizardfolk** army + **giant crocodile** (SRD)
  led by a **lizardfolk king** (new monster — CR 6).
- **The Wyvern's Lair.** **Wyvern** (SRD) protecting a captured ally.

### Chapter 5 — War of the River Kings

- **Irovetti's Tournament.** **Gladiators** (SRD *veteran*) + **mage** + the
  **Champion of Pitax** (new monster — CR 10).
- **The River Kings War.** **Hill giant** + **ogres** (SRD) mass combat — often
  resolved via kingdom-building rules, not combat.
- **Irovetti's Treachery.** **Pitax Guard** (new monster — *veteran* + extras,
  CR 4) + **succubus** advisor (SRD) and **King Irovetti** himself (new monster
  — CR 12 charismatic noble).

### Chapter 6 — Sound of a Thousand Screams

- **The Lantern King's Threshold.** **Will-o'-wisps** (SRD) and the **Horned
  Hunter** (new monster — Lantern King avatar, CR 14).
- **The Threshold.** **Satyr** + **dryad** and the **Nyrissa** encounter (new
  monster — nymph turned Eldest-pretender, CR 17).
- **The First World Itself.** **Doppelganger**, **pixie**, **quickling** swarms
  (SRD) defending the First World citadel.
- **The Lantern King (finale).** **The Lantern King** himself (new monster —
  Eldest of the First World, CR 19) with **First World spawn** mobs.

## Confidence notes

- Chapter structure confirmed via PathfinderWiki (authoritative for Paizo
  Pathfinder content).
- Kingmaker has an *official* 5e conversion published by Paizo in October
  2022, with a companion Kingmaker Bestiary (5E) that includes stat blocks
  for many of the monsters flagged above (Auchs the Stag Lord, Irovetti,
  Nyrissa, the Lantern King, etc.).
- The author agent should *preferentially* transcribe the Paizo-published
  5e stat blocks from the 2022 hardcover and bestiary rather than
  converting from Pathfinder 1e.
- The "Tartuccio" kobold is a Pathfinder IP but a generic kobold stat
  block suffices in 5e if the author chooses not to invest in a custom
  monster for the recurring minor villain.
