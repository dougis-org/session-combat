# Age of Worms — Research

## Adventure structure

12-chapter *Dungeon* magazine Adventure Path (July 2005 – June 2006), running levels 1–20 in the World of Greyhawk. Path published in Dungeon issues #124–135. Pilot for the format later used in Shackled City / Savage Tide / Scales of War. Final boss fight is the dracolich Dragotha and his master, the worm-god Kyuss.

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Diamond Lake: Whispering Cairn
- **Cultist Descent into the Whispering Cairn.** Adventurers explore the necromancer Eravan's cairn; first encounter with worm-cult acolytes beneath the tomb. Monsters: cultists of Kyuss (use **CM-vecna-cultist** stat block as analog + custom worm-swarm), ghoul patrol, wraith of the cairn's tomb guardian.

### Ch 4 — Hall of Harsh Reflections
- **The Doppelganger Cabal in the Free City.** Mind-flayed doppelganger assassins stalk the party through the Free City's noble quarter. Monsters: doppelgangers (SRD), mind flayer envoy (use **SRD Mind Flayer** stat block).

### Ch 6 — Gathering of Winds: Diamond Lake Returns
- **Battle of Diamond Lake — Dragon Assault.** Ilthane the blue dragon attacks Allustan's tower. Heroes defend the rebuilt cairn and re-enter to rescue the wizard. Monsters: young blue dragon (SRD), cultist warband.

### Ch 7 — Spire of Long Shadows
- **The Undying Dragon at Balakarde's Spire.** The alien architect of the Age of Worms awaits: an ancient undying dragon guards the spire where Balakarde's spirit is broken. Monsters: **CM-deathwolf** (pack of 3) hunting the flanks, beholder-eye tyrant in the upper chambers, custom undying dragon boss.

### Ch 9 — Isle of Last Resort
- **Dragotha's Lair Hidden in the Library.** Cleric of Vecna and his minions race the party for the phylactery. Monsters: **CM-necromancer-wizard** (Vecna cleric), **CM-vecna-cultist** warband, a night hag, and a hidden spawn of Dragotha (use dracolich analog: ancient blue dracolich).

### Ch 11 — Wormcrawl Fissure
- **The Balakarde Spirit Restoration.** Re-uniting the broken spirit of the prophet Balakarde in the worm-haunted fissure. Monsters: purple worms (SRD), worm-cult fanatics led by a Spawn of Kyuss.

### Ch 12 — Dawn of a New Age
- **Dragotha & Kyuss at Alhaster.** Final showdown — the dracolich Dragotha ascends to godhood and Kyuss manifests. Monsters: ancient blue dracolich (SRD analog), Kyuss-spawn heralds, and the Kyuss avatar (use **CM-vecna** + dracolich stat block as placeholder BBEG).

## New monsters needed

The Age of Worms is named for Kyuss and his worm-swarm cult. None of these monsters exist in the SRD. Required stat blocks (per Vecna-pattern, custom):

| ID | Name | CR | Source analog |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cm-kyuss-spawn` | Kyuss Spawn (medium worm-themed undead) | 5 | 3.5e MM |
| `cm-worm-that-walks` | Worm That Walks (human host body inhabited by parasitic worm-soul) | 6 | 3.5e MM |
| `cm-apostle-of-kyuss` | Apostle of Kyuss (cult cleric with worm-themed abilities) | 9 | 3.5e MM |
| `cm-dragotha` | Dragotha, the Dracolich | 17 | 3.5e MM (ancient blue dracolich) |
| `cm-kyuss` | Kyuss, the Worm-God (avatar aspect) | 22 | 3.5e Deities & Demigods |

Use existing SRD analogs for everything else: human cultists → SRD cultist, doppelgangers → SRD doppelganger, mind flayer → SRD mind flayer, beholder → SRD beholder, purple worm → SRD purple worm, blue dragon → SRD adult/blue dragon.
