# The Keep on the Borderlands — Research

## Adventure structure

B2 module (1979) by Gary Gygax, included in the *D&D Basic Set* printings 6–11 (1979–1982). Levels 1–3. Set in a generic D&D borderland location. Designed for new players and DMs. Ranked 7th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004). Used as the test adventure for the *Heroes of the Borderlands* 2024 starter set.

For G5 we treat the B2 module as a **3-chapter sampling**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Keep Itself | 1–2 | The Keep on the Borderlands |
| 2 | The Caves of Chaos (outer caves) | 1–3 | Caves of Chaos (24 humanoid caves) |
| 3 | The Caves of Chaos (inner sanctum / Zargon) | 3 | Inner Caves of Chaos (high-CR denizens) |

## Encounter plan (3-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — The Keep
- **The Treacherous Priest Encounter at the Keep.** A priest of chaotic evil secretly serves as a contact for the humanoid tribes. Monsters: **CM-b2-priest-of-evil** (custom CR-3 chaotic evil priest), bandits (SRD).

### Ch 2 — Caves of Chaos (Outer Caves)
- **Goblin, Orc, Hobgoblin, and Kobold Tribes.** 24 caves arranged around a central ravine. Each cave contains a different humanoid tribe. Monsters: goblin (SRD), orc (SRD), hobgoblin (SRD), kobold (SRD), bugbear (SRD), **CM-lizardfolk-ambush** (SRD lizardfolk).

### Ch 3 — Inner Caves of Chaos (Zargon)
- **Zargon, the One-Eyed Giant Evil.** The unifying evil intelligence behind the Caves of Chaos. Monsters: **CM-zargon-evil-giant-eye** (custom CR-8 one-eyed chaotic evil tyrant), ogre (SRD), gnoll (SRD), gnoll pack lord (SRD).
