# Courts of the Shadow Fey — Research

## Adventure structure

Third-party 5e mega-adventure by Kobold Press (creator of Tales of the Valiant / Black Flag). Originally published for Pathfinder and converted to 5e. Levels 1–14 (the 5e version begins around level 6 and runs to ~14, but earlier material can be added). Set in the Shadow Realm of the Shadow Fey — an otherworldly court of fey where political intrigue, betrayal, and romance dominate. The campaign is split between a dangerous descent into the Shadow Realm and political faction-play at the court.

For G5 we treat it as a **4-chapter mega-dungeon sampling**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entering the Shadow Realm | 6–8 | The Descent (mortal world → Shadow) |
| 2 | The Outer Courts | 8–10 | Politics & exploration |
| 3 | The Twisting Halls | 10–12 | Deeper court intrigue |
| 4 | The Heart of the Court | 12–14 | BBEG confrontation |

Final villain: The ruling monarch of the Shadow Fey court (a powerful archfey). The Shadow Fey themselves are not inherently evil — the campaign revolves around political faction-play and the corruption of the court's ruler.

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Entering the Shadow Realm
- **The Descent into Shadow.** A portal ritual takes the party to the Shadow Realm. Monsters: **CM-shadow-portal-guardian` (custom CR-8)** (analog: shadar-kai warrior + shadow abilities).

### Ch 2 — The Outer Courts
- **Political Ambush at the First Court.** Shadow Fey courtiers test the party with riddles and combat. Monsters: **CM-shadow-fey-courtier` (custom CR-5)** (use SRD nymph stat block + shadow magic).

### Ch 3 — The Twisting Halls
- **Shadow Fey Maze.** The court physically shifts; shadow fey warriors and shadow elementals. Monsters: **CM-shadow-fey-warrior` (custom CR-6)**, **CM-shadow-elemental` (custom CR-7)** (use SRD shadow analog + elemental traits).

### Ch 4 — The Heart of the Court
- **The Archfey Monarch.** Final boss. Monsters: **CM-archfey-monarch` (custom CR-15)**, **CM-shadow-fey-champion` (custom CR-10)** (use SRD druid stat block + shadow fey abilities), winter wolf (SRD).
