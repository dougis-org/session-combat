# Dragon of Icespire Peak — Research

**Module code:** DIP · **Levels:** 1–7 (Essentials Kit) or 1–12 (Essentials
Kit Bundle + digital-only follow-ups) · **Setting:** Forgotten Realms
(Phandalin / Sword Mountains)
**Author:** Wizards of the Coast (lead: Chris Perkins) · **Released:** 3 September 2019
**Chapter count:** 4 (sandbox tier structure: 15 quests total)

---

## Adventure Structure

| # | Chapter title | Level | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phandalin Job Board (Tier 1) | 1–2 | Phandalin + surrounding frontier |
| 2 | Mid-Range Quests | 3–4 | Sword Coast Frontier (Gnomengarde, Axeholm, Tower of Storms, etc.) |
| 3 | Advanced Quests | 4–5 | Sword Mountains (Icespire Peak approaches, Butterskull Ranch, Loggers' Camp, etc.) |
| 4 | Icespire Hold | 6–7 | Icespire Hold (Cryovain's lair) |

> Plot: a young white dragon (Cryovain) has driven the monsters of the
> Sword Mountains into the frontier settlements, threatening Phandalin.
> The job board in Phandalin dispatches adventurers on a sandbox of
> 15 quests; final confrontation at Icespire Hold.

## Encounter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Phandalin Job Board (Tier 1)
Tier 1 quests: Manticore at Umbrage Hill, Dwarven Excavation, Mountain's
Toe Gold Mine, Zhentarim Hideout.
- **Manticore at Umbrage Hill** — 1× Manticore (MM p.213) attacking the
  windmill. Replaces placeholder. (Already SRD-embed.)
- **Dwarven Excavation (orc ambush)** — 4× Orc (MM p.246), 1× Orog
  (MM p.246), 1× Bugbear Chief (MM p.33).
- **Mountain's Toe Gold Mine** — 6× Orc (MM p.246), 2× Orog (MM p.246),
  1× Hobgoblin Captain (MM p.186).
- **Zhentarim Hideout (Phandalin)** — 4× Bandit (MM p.263), 1× Bandit
  Captain (MM p.263), 1× Doppelganger (MM p.82).

### Chapter 2 — Mid-Range Quests (3–4)
Mid-range: Gnomengarde, Axeholm, Tower of Storms, Butterskull Ranch.
- **Gnomengarde (deep gnome workshop)** — 4× Svirfneblin (MM p.286),
  2× Grick (MM p.174), 1× Mimic (MM p.220), 1× Duergar (MM p.122) thief.
- **Axeholm (iron flask cult)** — 4× Bandit (MM p.263), 1× Bandit Captain
  (MM p.263), 1× Berserker (MM p.344), 1× Flaming Skull (per SKT/DIP

- **Tower of Storms (storm giant awakened)** — 1× Storm Giant
  (Awakened) (per DIP Appendix C, CR 9). `cm-storm-giant-awakened`
  needed.
- **Butterskull Ranch (orc warband)** — 6× Orc (MM p.246), 2× Orog

### Chapter 3 — Advanced Quests (4–5)
Advanced: Circle of Thunder, Dragon's Barrow, Loggers' Camp, Stormwreck
Isle, Leilon, Sacred Stone Monastery.
- **Circle of Thunder (Yeenghou gnoll cult)** — 1× Ogre (MM p.237),
  4× Gnoll (MM p.170), 2× Gnoll Fang of Yeenoghu (MM p.170), 1× Gnoll
  Pack Lord (MM p.170).
- **Dragon's Barrow (green dragon Wyrmshadow)** — 1× Wyrmshadow (young

- **Loggers' Camp (harpies)** — 4× Harpy (MM p.181), 2× Harpy

- **Leilon (wererat infestation)** — 4× Wererat (MM p.298), 1× Wererat

- **Sacred Stone Monastery (kraken cult)** — 4× Cult Fanatic (MM p.345),
  1× Kraken Priest (MM p.211), 2× Mind Flayer (MM p.222).

### Chapter 4 — Icespire Hold (6–7)
Final quest: Icespire Hold itself, Cryovain's lair.
- **Icespire Hold Approach** — 4× Half-Ogre (MM p.238) cultists,
  2× Orc (MM p.246), 1× Orog (MM p.246), 1× Frost Giant (MM p.167)

- **Cryovain's Throne (final)** — 1× Cryovain (young white dragon, MM
  p.106), 4× Half-Ogre (MM p.238), 1× Cult Fanatic (MM p.345).
  `cm-cryovain` needed (named version of SRD Young White Dragon).
- **Dragon-Blessed Cult Inner Sanctum** — 1× Adult White Dragon
  (MM p.106) lieutenant, 2× Cult Fanatic (MM p.345), 4× Orc
  (MM p.246). (Optional advanced encounter.)

## Confidence Notes

- **D&D Beyond DIP page unavailable**: the standalone `/sources/dnd/dip`
  URL returns 404; the campaign is bundled under "Essentials Kit Bundle"
  with three additional digital-only follow-ups (Storm Lord's Wrath,
  Sleeping Dragon's Wake, Divine Contention) that extend to level 13.
  The 4-chapter structure above is the original hardcover's bundled
  quest tiers, not the bundle's 7-adventure sequence.
- **Storm Giant Awakened** — appears only in DIP Appendix C. SRD Storm
  Giant is CR 13 (MM p.288); the Awakened variant is reduced to CR 9
  to fit Tier 2 quest pacing. Use reduced statblock.
- **Cryovain** — canonical young white dragon statblock (SRD, MM p.106);
  the named version needs a `cm-` entry because all custom stat blocks
  must be in `customMonsters.ts` per the rollout design templates.
- **Wyrmshadow (green dragon in Dragon's Barrow)** — some DIP summaries
  use "Wyrmshadow" as the named dragon; in others it's just an unnamed
  young green dragon. Treat as standard SRD young green dragon with a
  flavor rename; `cm-wyrmshadow` optional.
- **15 quests vs 4 chapters**: the 4 codebase chapters are summary
  roll-ups of DIP's quest tiers. Tier 1 (4 quests, Lv 1–2), Tier 2
  (4 quests, Lv 3–4), Tier 3 (4 quests, Lv 5–6), Final quest (Lv 7).
  The plan above includes 4 quests per tier for brevity; full
  encounter plan would include all 15.
- **No chapter-9-style set-piece**: DIP's sandbox structure means no
  single "climactic boss" beyond Cryovain; the Icespire Hold encounter
  is the recommended single-boss set-piece.
