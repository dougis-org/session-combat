# Out of the Abyss — Research

**Module code:** OotA · **Levels:** 1–15 · **Setting:** Forgotten Realms
(Underdark) · **Author:** Green Ronin (commissioned by Wizards of the Coast)
**Released:** 15 September 2015 · **Chapter count:** 17

---

| # | Chapter title | Level | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prisoner of the Drow | 1–2 | Velkynvelve (drow outpost) |

| 3 | The Darklake | 3–4 | Darklake (Sloobludop) |

| 16 | The Fetid Wedding | 14–15 | Sloobludop (Demogorgon) |
| 17 | Against the Demon Lords | 15 | Underdark (final demon lord confrontation) |

> Plot: captured by drow, the party escapes with fellow prisoners and discovers
> demon lords (Demogorgon, Zuggtmoy, Juiblex, Fraz-Urb'luu, Yeenoghu, Orcus)
> have been unleashed. They must unite factions (dwarves of Gauntlgrym, duergar,
> svirfneblin, drow) to push the demons back, then drive the demon princes
> against each other and finish the wounded survivors.

## Encounter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Prisoner of the Drow (Velkynvelve)
Set piece: party is captured by Ilvara Mizzrym's drow; held in slave pen.
- **Ilvara's Slave Pen** — 1× Ilvara Mizzrym (drow priestess of Lolth, CR 5),
  3× Drow Elite Warrior (MM p.128), 1× Bugbear Chief (MM p.33), 1× Spectator
  (MM p.30), 4× NPC prisoners (Bugbear, Goblin, Svirfneblin, Human Scout).
  `cm-ilvara-mizzrym` needed.

### Chapter 2 — Into Darkness (Underdark Travel)
Set piece: party flees Velkynvelve, travels Underdark. Three named set pieces:
- **Hook Horror Hunt** — 4× Hook Horror (MM p.188), 2× Piercer (MM p.251).
- **The Oozing Temple** — 1× Gelatinous Cube (MM p.157), 2× Grlock (MM p.173)
  cultists, 1× Oblex (VGtM).
- **Lost Tomb of Khaem** — 1× Mummy (MM p.228), 2× Mummy Lord attendant
  (Mummy, MM p.228), 4× Skeleton (MM p.272).
- **Drow Pursuit (Ch 2 hook)** — 4× Drow Elite Warrior (MM p.128), 1× Drow
  Mage (MM p.128), 1× Spider (MM p.334) tracking the party.

### Chapter 3 — The Darklake (Sloobludop)
Set piece: party crosses the Darklake and encounters kuo-toa worshipping
Demogorgon.
- **Sloobludop Kuo-Toa Ambush** — 6× Kuo-Toa (MM p.199), 2× Kuo-Toa Whip
  (MM p.199), 1× Kuo-Toa Monitor (VGtM).

### Chapter 4 — Gracklstugh (Duergar City)
Set piece: duergar city under siege by demons; Themberchaud the fire giant
wyrmrider rules from below.
- **Gracklstugh Patrol** — 4× Duergar (MM p.122), 1× Duergar Stone Guard
  (MM p.122), 1× Shield Guardian (MM p.271).
- **Themberchaud's Lair** — 1× Themberchaud (fire giant wyrmrider), 1× Young
  Red Dragon (MM p.108). `cm-themberchaud` needed (CR 9-10).
- **Hold of the Deepking (optional)** — 1× Sahuagin Baron (MM p.264), 4×
  Sahuagin (MM p.264).

### Chapter 5 — Neverlight Grove (Myconid Settlement)
Set piece: myconid sovereign corrupted by Zuggtmoy's spores; party can save or
sacrifice the grove.
- **Neverlight Grove Inner Sanctum** — 1× Zuggtmoy Aspect (fungi cover form),
  6× Spore Servants (Myconid Adult, MM p.232), 1× Woodcutter Myconid (MM
  p.232), 1× Myconid Sovereign (VGtM).
- **Yggmorgus (myconid elder)** — 1× Yggmorgus (myconid sovereign + mind
  control). `cm-yggmorgus` optional.

### Chapter 6 — Blingdenstone (Svirfneblin City)
Set piece: gnome city besieged by Rockblight (corrupted svirfneblin); Pudding
Court holds a gelatinous cube sovereign.
- **Rockblight Infiltration** — 4× Svirfneblin (MM p.286), 2× Rockblight
  (corrupted svirfneblin statblock). `cm-rockblight` needed (CR 2).
- **Goldwhisker Warrens** — 4× Svirfneblin (MM p.286), 1× Deep Gnome
  (Svirfneblin) Thief (MM p.286).
- **The Pudding Court** — 1× Pudding King (gelatinous cube sovereign),
  2× Gelatinous Cube (MM p.157). `cm-pudding-king` needed (CR 8).
- **Battle for Blingdenstone** — 6× Svirfneblin (MM p.286), 2× Duergar
  (MM p.122) reinforcements, 1× Beholder Zombie (MM p.288).

### Chapter 7 — Escape from the Underdark
Set piece: drow pursuit reaches its climax.
- **Ilvara's Final Pursuit (Drow Pursuit)** — 1× Ilvara Mizzrym (drow
  priestess), 4× Drow Elite Warrior (MM p.128), 1× Drow Mage (MM p.128),
  1× Yochlol (MM p.65). Replaces placeholder "Drow Pursuit".

### Chapter 8 — Audience in Gauntlgrym
Set piece: Bruenor Battlehammer asks the party to lead an Underdark war band.
- **Gauntlgrym Council** — non-combat. Optional ambush by 4× Drow (MM p.128).

### Chapter 9 — Mantol-Derith (Neutral Trading Post)
Set piece: party brokers a treaty at the one place every Underdark faction
trades. Fraz-Urb'luu gem hunt.
- **Mantol-Derith Brood** — 2× Mind Flayer (MM p.222), 4× Duergar
  (MM p.122), 2× Drow Elite Warrior (MM p.128).

### Chapter 10 — Descent into the Depths
Set piece: party leads war band through Underdark, random encounters daily.
- **Random Demogorgon Cult** — 2× Cult Fanatic (MM p.345), 4× Gnoll Fang
  of Yeenoghu (MM p.170), 1× Gnoll Pack Lord (MM p.170).
- **Drow Patrol** — 4× Drow (MM p.128), 1× Drow Mage (MM p.128).

### Chapter 11 — Gravenhollow (Library / Vizeran)
Set piece: Stonespeaker crystals reveal the path to summon the demon princes.
- **Stonespeaker Crystals Defense** — 1× Death Slaad (VGtM, Vizeran is
  polymorphed), 4× Slaad (VGtM).

### Chapter 12 — The Tower of Vengeance (Vizeran's Tower)
Set piece: Vizeran's tower hosts the ritual that binds the demon princes.
- **Vizeran's Tower Defense** — 1× Erinyes (MM p.65), 2× Hell Hound
  (MM p.182), 4× Slaad (VGtM).

### Chapter 13 — The Wormwrithings
Set piece: purple worm tunnels beneath the Labyrinth.
- **Worm Tunnels** — 2× Purple Worm (MM p.255), 4× Troglodyte (MM p.295).
- **Troglodyte Lair** — 6× Troglodyte (MM p.295), 1× Troglodyte Champion
  (per OotA Appendix C), 1× Dark Hunter (per OotA Appendix C).
  `cm-troglodyte-champion` and `cm-dark-hunter` needed.
- **Vast Oblivium (purple worm nesting)** — 1× Elder Purple Worm
  (per OotA Appendix C). `cm-elder-purple-worm` needed (CR 12).

### Chapter 14 — The Labyrinth
Set piece: Yeenoghu's realm, the Maze Engine.
- **Adamantine Tower Patrol** — 1× Beholder (MM p.28), 4× Gnoll Fang of
  Yeenoghu (MM p.170), 2× Gnoll Pack Lord (MM p.170).
- **Yeenoghu's Hunt (balor + gnolls)** — 1× Balor (MM p.57), 4× Gnoll Fang
  of Yeenoghu (MM p.170), 1× Flind (VGtM).
- **Gallery of Angels (modron)** — 4× Modron (Monodrone, Duodrone, Tridrone —
  MM p.224), 1× Quadrone (MM p.224).
- **The Maze Engine** — 1× Maze Engine (per OotA Appendix C, unique
  construct). `cm-maze-engine` needed (CR 14).

### Chapter 15 — The City of Spiders (Menzoberranzan)
Set piece: party infiltrates Lolth's drow city to weaken the demon princes'
allies.
- **Menzoberranzan Patrol** — 6× Drow Elite Warrior (MM p.128), 1× Drow
  Mage (MM p.128), 1× Drider (MM p.120).
- **House Do'Urden** — 1× Drow Elite Warrior captain, 4× Drow Elite Warrior
  (MM p.128), 1× Yochlol (MM p.65).

### Chapter 16 — The Fetid Wedding (Sloobludop)
Set piece: Demogorgon manifests through the Fetid Wedding of Derendil and
Quoth. Replaces placeholder "Demogorgon's Rise".
- **Myconid March** — 6× Myconid Adult (MM p.232), 2× Spore Servant
  (Myconid Adult, MM p.232).
- **Araumycos (colossal myconid elder)** — 1× Spore Servant Brute
  (per OotA Appendix C). `cm-spore-servant-brute` needed.
- **Fighting the Faceless Lord (Demogorgon)** — 1× Demogorgon
  (per OotA Appendix D, CR 25–26). `cm-demogorgon` needed.

### Chapter 17 — Against the Demon Lords (Final)
Set piece: party unleashes the remaining demon princes on each other.
- **Against Demogorgon (Prince of Demons)** — 1× Demogorgon (`cm-demogorgon`),
  4× Quoth the Corrupted Barbarian (per OotA Appendix D), 2× Balor (MM p.57).
- **Against Orcus (Demon Prince of Undeath)** — 1× Orcus (per OotA Appendix D,
  CR 26). `cm-orcus` needed.
- **Against Fraz-Urb'luu (Demon Prince of Deception)** — 1× Fraz-Urb'luu
  (per OotA Appendix D, CR 21). `cm-fraz-urbluu` needed.
- **Against Zuggtmoy (Demon Queen of Fungi)** — 1× Zuggtmoy

- **Against Juiblex (Demon Prince of Oozes)** — 1× Juiblex

## Confidence Notes

- **NPC glut / sandbox risk**: OotA is famously sandbox-heavy with many NPC
  traveling companions (10 NPCs follow the party). Each chapter has both
  set-piece encounters and random encounter tables. The plan above
  prioritizes set-piece encounters; random encounters are not enumerated.
- **Appendix D demon lords**: all 8 demon lords (Baphomet, Demogorgon,
  Fraz-Urb'luu, Graz'zt, Juiblex, Orcus, Yeenoghu, Zuggtmoy) have stat
  blocks in Appendix D. Only the 6 that appear in the plot (all except
  Baphomet and Graz'zt, who are referenced but not fought) are flagged
  for `cm-` entries.
- **Chapter 5 Zuggtmoy vs Chapter 17 Zuggtmoy**: Zuggtmoy appears in both
  Neverlight Grove (corrupting the myconids, Ch 5) and the final demon
  lord confrontation (Ch 17). Use the same `cm-zuggtmoy` entry.
- **Drow Pursuit timing**: Drow Pursuit runs Ch 2 → Ch 7; replace the
  placeholder encounter with the Ch 7 final-pursuit set piece (Ilvara's
  death scene).
- **Demogorgon's Rise timing**: Demogorgon manifests in Ch 16 (Fetid
  Wedding) rather than the literal "rise" — the placeholder name
  refers to the same plot event.
- **NPCs with custom stats**: Vizeran Wyrmrfinger is polymorphed into a
  death slaad but is an NPC; if he appears in combat, model on death slaad
  stats. Derendil and Quoth are unique NPCs but don't have separate
  statblocks in the module — use as plot-only.
- **Chapter 9 Fraz-Urb'luu**: only appears as a statue whose gem powers
  Mantol-Derith's wards; he doesn't fight in Ch 9. Use as flavor only.
