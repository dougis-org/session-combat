# Tales from the Yawning Portal — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `TYP`
**Type:** Anthology (7 classic-module conversions)
**Source:** WotC 5e, March 2017 (ISBN 978-0-7869-6609-7)
**Levels:** 1 → "high" (Tomb of Horrors is for high-level characters)
**Setting:** Setting-agnostic; each adventure can be dropped into any campaign.

## Adventure structure (7 chapters)

| # | Chapter | Level | Original module (year) | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sunless Citadel | 1 | 3e (2000) | Classic dungeon crawl; goblin fruiting tree. |
| 2 | The Forge of Fury | 3 | 3e (2000) | Dwarven-fortress dungeon. |
| 3 | The Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan | 5 | AD&D (1980) | Mesoamerican-themed megadungeon. |
| 4 | White Plume Mountain | 8 | AD&D (1979) | Wacky artifact heist. |
| 5 | Dead in Thay | 9 | D&D Next playtest (2014) | Mega-dungeon crawl. |
| 6 | Against the Giants | 11 | AD&D (1981) | Three-part giant lairs. |
| 7 | Tomb of Horrors | "high" | AD&D (1978) | Tournament-style deathtrap dungeon. |

## Encounter plan

One signature encounter per chapter — usually the chapter's named set-piece boss.

- **1 · The Sunless Citadel** — *Belak's Goblin Court.* **Goblin** warband led
  by **Belak the Sunless** (SRD *goblin boss* + necromancer trait).
- **2 · The Forge of Fury** — *Dwarven Forge Defense.* **Duergar**, **drow
  elite**, and the **forge dragon** (new monster — young red dragon under
  magical control) guarding the lost dwarven forge.
- **3 · Hidden Shrine of Tamoachan** — *Tamoachan Tomb.* **Shambling mound**,
  **grick alpha**, **green slime** traps; final chamber is the **Shrine
  Guardian** (new monster — mummy lord variant).
- **4 · White Plume Mountain** — *The Three Weapons.* **Roper**, **cleric of
  Nerull**, and the three named artifacts (Wave, Whelm, Blackrazor). Boss is
  **Keraptis** the mad archmage (new monster — archmage + greater invisibility).
- **5 · Dead in Thay** — *The Doomvault.* This is a mega-dungeon with 21
  chambers. The standout encounter is the **Belhaim Three** — three green
  hag coven lords (SRD). Final boss is **Szass Tam** (substitute for SRD
  *red wizard* + lich template; new monster recommended).
- **6 · Against the Giants** — *Three-Giant Climax.* Three sequential bosses:
  **King Snurre** (SRD *hill giant* lord), **Queen Snarla** (SRD *stone giant*
  lord), **King Hekaton** (SRD *frost giant* lord, mistaken ally). Also
  includes **Fomorian king** subboss.
- **7 · Tomb of Horrors** — *Acererak's Trap.* This is mostly a puzzle/trap
  dungeon. The only meaningful combat is **Acererak** the demilich himself
  (substitute SRD *demilich* — open-licensed version available via 5etools).
  Encounter: skeleton + wraith swarms + Acererak.

## New monsters needed

All the chapter-finale monsters either exist in the SRD or can be substituted
with SRD templates. The author agent has flexibility here. Only a few really
need new custom stat blocks.

| cm- id | Name | CR (est.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cm-typ-keraptis` | Keraptis, the Mad Archmage | 12 | Sub-boss of White Plume Mountain. |
| `cm-typ-szass-tam` | Szass Tam, the Red Wizard | 16 | Zulkir of Necromancy; Dead in Thay finale. |
| `cm-typ-forge-dragon` | Forge Dragon (variant) | 7 | Mind-controlled young red dragon in Forge of Fury. |
| `cm-typ-shrine-guardian` | Hidden Shrine Guardian | 8 | Mummy-lord variant at Tamoachan's heart. |

(Recommendation: most encounters can be covered by SRD substitutions — Yawning
Portal is famous for the *classic* AD&D monster roster, all of which have
5e equivalents in the SRD or on 5etools.)

## Confidence notes

- Chapter list and level ranges are confirmed via Wikipedia (which cites
  the official WotC TYP Introductory PDF, archived by Wizards).
- Most chapter bodies are AD&D / 3e originals, so encounter design
  leverages the WotC 5e conversions (open-source SRD substitutes via 5etools
  for monsters like Acererak the demilich, mind flayers, etc.).
- "Tomb of Horrors" is widely noted as the *standout* of the anthology by
  reviewers but also the *deadliest* — design recommendation: keep it as a
  late-campaign finale.
