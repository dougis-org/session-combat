# Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen — Research

## Adventure structure

5e hardcover adventure (2022) by Justice Ramin Arman, Andrew Brooks, and Clare Dugmore. Levels 1–11. Set on Krynn during the opening of the War of the Lance (Dragonlance setting). The Dragon Queen Takhisis invades Solamnia with the Blue Lady (a corrupted Tiamat avatar) and her army of draconians, led by death knight Lord Soth. Companion board game: *Dragonlance: Warriors of Krynn*.

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prelude / Termination Dust | 1 | Solamnia (before war) |
| 2 | The War of the Lance Begins | 2–3 | Solamnia front |
| 3 | Sanction's Warning | 4–5 | Kalaman / Sanction |
| 4 | On the Road | 5–6 | Solamnia countryside |
| 5 | Soth's Advance | 7–8 | Battle of the Plains of Solamnia |
| 6 | The Flying Citadels | 9 | High Skies of Krynn (sky vehicles) |
| 7 | Final Confrontation | 10–11 | Sanction / Soth's Citadel |

Final villain: Lord Soth, Death Knight of the Rose (a recurring Dragonlance antagonist). Companion content includes the Lunar Sorcery sorcerer subclass and kender as a playable race.

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Prelude (Termination Dust)
- **Evacuation of the Village of Hommlet-equivalent.** Village burns; refugees flee before the dragon army arrives. Monsters: **CM-draconian-foot-soldier** (custom CR-1/2 hobgoblin-dragon hybrid), **CM-dragon-army-officer** (custom CR-3), flames licking the fields.

### Ch 2–3 — Sanction's Warning
- **Draconian Patrol Ambush on the Solamnia Road.** Mixed patrol of sivak (shape-shifting), aurak (lightning), and kapak (poison) draconians. Monsters: **CM-draconian-sivak** (CR-3 shapeshifter), **CM-draconian-aurak** (CR-3 lightning), **CM-draconian-kapak** (CR-3 poison).

### Ch 4 — On the Road
- **Blue Lady's Vanguard.** The corrupted Tiamat aspect leads an advance strike; female blue dragon leads knights. Monsters: **CM-blue-dragon-vv** (custom CR-8 young blue dragon), **CM-blue-dragon-v-v-knight** (custom CR-5), hobgoblin auxiliaries.

### Ch 5 — Soth's Advance (Battle of the Plains)
- **Lord Soth Confronts the Heroes.** The Death Knight commands the central host on the plains. Monsters: **CM-lord-soth-death-knight** (custom CR-16 death knight — *note: exists in Vecna roster too*), skeletal knights (SRD), skeletal soldiers (SRD).

### Ch 6 — Flying Citadels
- **Sky-Ship Boarding Action.** Heroes assault one of Takhisis's flying citadels. Monsters: **CM-nehamendian-dragonarmy-leader** (custom CR-12 dragon-army commander), gargoyles (SRD), hobgoblin captains (SRD).

### Ch 7 — Final Confrontation at Sanction
- **Lord Soth's Last Stand.** Final fight against Soth and the Blue Lady in Sanction. Monsters: **CM-lord-soth-death-knight** (full stats), **CM-blue-lady-tiamat-aspect** (custom CR-15 blue dragon Tiamat aspect), skeletal host.
