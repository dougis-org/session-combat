# Spelljammer: Adventures in Space — Light of Xaryxis — Research

## Adventure structure

5e Spelljammer hardcover adventure (2022) by Justice Ramin Arman and Andrew Darrow, included in *Spelljammer: Adventures in Space*. Levels 1–8. Set in the Astral Sea / Wildspace / Xaryxis sphere. The empire of Xaryxis (a race of mind flayers in a mind-controlled illithid empire) is building a beacon to attack Toril, and the heroes must travel the Rock of Bral to Xaryxis to stop them.

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Message in a Bottle | 1–2 | Rock of Bral (introduction to space travel) |
| 2 | Below the Rock | 3–4 | Underdark of Toril (gateway) |
| 3 | Wildspace Encounter | 5 | Wildspace / Xaryxis sphere approaches |
| 4 | The Xaryxian Empire | 6–8 | Xaryxis capital |

Final villain: **Xhalcaraz**, Xaryxis's emperor (a mind flayer variant). Allies include the vampirate captain Hallothere Lazybower.

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — Message in a Bottle / Rock of Bral
- **First Spelljammer Crew Adventure.** Players meet the Rock of Bral pirate council and are recruited. Monsters: **CM-svirfneblin** (deep gnome ally stat block), ordinary goblinoid crew, **CM-brallish-pirate-captain** (CR-2 humanoid NPC).

### Ch 2 — Below the Rock
- **Underdark Gateway with Githyanki Patrol.** Mind-controlled githyanki crew try to stop the heroes from activating the gateway. Monsters: githyanki warrior (SRD), githyanki mind mage (SRD), mind flayer thrall (use SRD mind flayer).

### Ch 3 — Wildspace Encounter
- **Vampirate Ambush in Wildspace.** Hallothere Lazybower and his vampirate crew close on the heroes' spelljammer. Monsters: **CM-vampirate** (custom CR-8 undead pirate; uses Energy Drain 30-ft ranged attack), shadows (SRD).

### Ch 4 — The Xaryxian Empire
- **Xhalcaraz, Emperor of Xaryxis.** The mind flayer emperor has bonded to a god-like cosmic entity. Monsters: **CM-xhalcaraz` actually ultroloth** (custom CR-13 ultroloth, mind flayer empire commander), mind flayers (SRD), **CM-mind-controlled-paladin** (custom CR-5 thrall).
