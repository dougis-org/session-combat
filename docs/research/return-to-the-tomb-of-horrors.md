# Return to the Tomb of Horrors — Research

## Adventure structure

2e AD&D boxed set (1998) by Bruce R. Cordell. Levels 13–16. Sequel to the original 1978 S1 *Tomb of Horrors* by Gary Gygax. Set in Greyhawk, picks up where the original Tomb left off — the original was merely an antechamber to Acererak's true resting place. The dust from the destroyed skull demilich opens a way to the cursed city of Moil in a pocket universe of eternal darkness, and beyond that to Acererak's fortress hovering at the edge of the Negative Energy Plane.

Won the 1998 Origins Award for Best Roleplaying Adventure. Ranked 10th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004).

For G5 we treat the boxed set as a **3-chapter sampling**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Tomb of Horrors (Original Rematch) | 13 | Original Tomb of Acererak (the antechamber) |
| 2 | The City of Moil | 14–15 | Pocket universe, dark city (introduced in this sequel) |
| 3 | Acererak's Fortress | 15–16 | Fortress on the edge of the Negative Energy Plane |

Final villain: **Acererak the Demilich** (now revealed as a true multiversal threat seeking godhood through soul consumption). Heroes must prevent him from completing the ritual.

## Encounter plan (3-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — The Tomb of Horrors (Original)
- **The Demilich Skull Destroyed.** Heroes revisit the original Tomb, defeat Acererak's decoy demilich form, smash the jade skull. Monsters: **CM-acererak-decoy-demilich** (custom CR-20 demilich), **CM-sphere-of-annihilation` actually** (use SRD lifedrinker or CR-7 living spell analog), lacedon (use SRD ghoul variant).

### Ch 2 — The City of Moil
- **Cursed Eternal-Darkness City.** Moil is a pocket universe of eternal night; inhabitants are tortured souls, and the city itself is in the grip of Acererak's soul-collecting apparatus. Monsters: **CM-cursed-moilian-wraith** (custom CR-9 incorporeal undead), **CM-night-prowler-shadow-demon** (custom CR-7 shadow demon), banshee (SRD).

### Ch 3 — Acererak's Fortress (Negative Energy Plane Edge)
- **Acererak, True Demilich.** Final boss — Acererak is on the verge of godhood, powered by souls consumed over millennia. Monsters: **CM-acererak-true-demilich` actually** (custom CR-22 demilich — *note: existing cm-acererak in Vecna roster is the archlich; this is the older demilich aspect; pick the one that fits the lore*), **CM-soul-monger-construct** (custom CR-10 construct), **CM-acolyte-of-acererak** (custom CR-6 cleric), skeletal dragon (SRD), nightshades (SRD), nighthuggers (SRD).
