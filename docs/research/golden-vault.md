# Keys from the Golden Vault — Research

**Module code (proposed):** `KGV`
**Type:** Anthology (13 heist-themed standalone adventures)
**Source:** WotC 5e, February 2023 (ISBN 9780786968961)
**Levels:** 1–11
**Setting:** Forgotten Realms (or any setting); the Golden Vault is a mysterious
just-cause thieves' guild acting as the unifying McGuffin.

## Adventure structure (13 chapters)

| # | Chapter | Level | Location / hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Murkmire Malevolence | 1 | Murder-mystery in a remote lizardfolk-infested manor. |
| 2 | The Stygian Gambit | 2 | Heist of an infamous underground casino (Hell-style). |
| 3 | Reach for the Stars | 3 | Break-in at an astronomical observatory. |
| 4 | Prisoner 13 | 4 | Infiltration of a magical prison. |
| 5 | Tockworth's Clockworks | 5 | Heist of an animated-construct factory. |
| 6 | Masterpiece Imbroglio | 5 | Theft of a magical portrait from a museum. |
| 7 | Axe from the Grave | 6 | Recovery of an ancestral axe from a barbarian crypt. |
| 8 | Vidorant's Vault | 7 | High-security bank vault. |
| 9 | Affair on the Concordant Express | 9 | Murder-mystery on a moving magical train. |
| 10 | Shard of the Accursed | 8 | Recovery of a cursed gemstone. |
| 11 | Heart of Ashes | 8 | Volcanic lair raid. |
| 12 | Party at Paliset Hall | 10 | High-society masquerade heist. |
| 13 | Fire and Darkness | 11 | Final heist against a fire giant stronghold. |

(Note: chapter ordering is the canonical table-of-contents order as confirmed
on Wikipedia and D&D Beyond. The Stygian Gambit is documented as both
"sandbox-heavy" and a frequent DM stumbling block per the Wargamer review.)

## Encounter plan

One key combat encounter per chapter. Because the anthology's theme is heists,
many chapters support either a stealth/social resolution *or* a violent option —
encounters below represent the violent-option finale.

- **1 · Murkmire Malevolence** — *Manor Cleanup.* **Lizardfolk** warband +
  **bullywug** sub-leader (SRD).
- **2 · Stygian Gambit** — *Casino Vault Break.* **Spined devils**, **dretch**,
  + **pit fiend** herald (SRD).
- **3 · Reach for the Stars** — *Observatory Defense.* **Cultists**,
  **Astromancy cadets** (SRD *acolyte* + flavor), and a **mind flayer**
  (SRD) mastermind.
- **4 · Prisoner 13** — *Magical Prison Break.* **Giant spiders**, **ettin**
  guards, **boneclaw** warden (SRD).
- **5 · Tockworth's Clockworks** — *Animated Factory Heist.* **Animated
  armor**, **warforged titan** (SRD *golem* flavor), **invisible stalker**
  (SRD).
- **6 · Masterpiece Imbroglio** — *Museum Gallery Fight.* **Doppelgangers**
  and a **gynosphinx** curator (SRD).
- **7 · Axe from the Grave** — *Crypt Defense.* **Wraiths**, **wight**,
  **barrow guardian** (SRD *boneclaw* substitute).
- **8 · Vidorant's Vault** — *High-Security Bank.* **Roc** at perimeter,
  **arcanaloth** vault guardian, **giff** mercenary squads (SRD).
- **9 · Affair on the Concordant Express** — *Murder on the Train.* **Assassins**,
  **spies**, **succubus** seductress (SRD). Highlighted by reviewers as the
  standout heist in the book.
- **10 · Shard of the Accursed** — *Cursed Gem Recovery.* **Death dogs**,
  **flameskulls**, **mummy** (SRD).
- **11 · Heart of Ashes** — *Volcanic Lair.* **Magmin**, **fire elemental**,
  **salamander** (SRD).
- **12 · Party at Paliset Hall** — *Masquerade Heist.* **Vampire spawn**,
  **dread knight** (SRD *death knight* substitute), and a **succubus** social
  infiltration target.
- **13 · Fire and Darkness** — *Fire-Giant Hold Raid.* **Fire giant**,
  **elder fire elemental**, **red dragon** (SRD).

## New monsters needed

Few unique monsters — most chapters use SRD creatures. The Golden Vault's
"Goldentouched" lieutenant class is occasionally flagged in reviews but no
named unique NPCs surface. Substitutes preferred over custom stat blocks.

| cm- id | Name | CR (est.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cm-kgv-goldentouched` | Golden Vault Lieutenant | 3 | Generic recurring heist-master. Optional — can use SRD *spy*. |

(Recommended: substitute SRD for almost every encounter. The heist theme
naturally lends itself to combat-light chapters.)

## Confidence notes

- Chapter list and level ranges confirmed via Wikipedia (which cites the WotC
  official product page).
- Wargamer's review reveals the heist mechanics are "shallow" — design
  recommendation: lean on SRD monsters and emphasise combat encounters
  representing the violent failure state of the heist.
- Because D&D 5e has no native heist subsystem, encounter design should focus
  on the *consequence* of failure rather than the heist itself.
