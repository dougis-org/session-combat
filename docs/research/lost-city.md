# The Lost City — Research

## Adventure structure

B4 module (1982) by Tom Moldvay. *D&D* Basic Set. Levels 1–3 (expandable to higher via *Expert* rules). Set on the edges of the Mystara campaign setting in a vast desert. Working title was "The Lost City of Cynidicea." Famous for being only partially detailed — Moldvay designed it as a low-level scenario to give novice DMs experience in fleshing out adventures. The lower pyramid and the underground city are sketched; the upper pyramid is fully detailed. The main villain is **Zargon**, a giant one-eyed monster. Ranked 28th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004). 5e remaster in *Quests from the Infinite Staircase* (2024). Goodman Games announced *Original Adventures Reincarnated #4: The Lost City* (2020).

For G5 we treat B4 as a **4-chapter sampling** reflecting the natural expansion path (upper pyramid → lower pyramid → city → Zargon):

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upper Pyramid (fully detailed) | 1–2 | Above-ground step pyramid |
| 2 | Lower Pyramid (partially detailed) | 2 | Below-ground level 1 |
| 3 | The Lost City of Cynidicea | 2–3 | Regressed subterranean city |
| 4 | Zargon's Lair (final) | 3 | Evil high priest / one-eyed god-monster |

## Encounter plan (4-chapter sampling)

### Ch 1 — Upper Pyramid (above ground)
- **Pyramid Mummy Lords.** Zargon-cult mummies guard the upper pyramid. Monsters: mummy (SRD), **CM-pyramid-mummy-lord** (custom CR-9 mummy lord), giant spider (SRD).

### Ch 2 — Lower Pyramid (partially detailed)
- **Cynidicean Masked Refugees.** Regressed subterranean dwellers in costumes; some are drug-addled and harmless, some are cult agents. Monsters: **CM-cynidicean-cult-rogue** (custom CR-1 chaotic humanoid), **CM-masked-cult-priest** (custom CR-3).

### Ch 3 — The Lost City of Cynidicea
- **Faction Conflict in the City.** Three factions of "normal" Cynidiceans (cultured, descended from the ancient city) vying for control. Monsters: **CM-cynidicean-priestess** (custom CR-5), **CM-cynidicean-faction-fighter** (CR-2), giant lizard (SRD), giant poisonous snake (SRD).

### Ch 4 — Zargon's Lair
- **Zargon, the One-Eyed Evil God-Monster.** Final BBEG. Monsters: **CM-zargon-one-eyed-god** (custom CR-10 one-eyed aberrant), **CM-priests-of-zargon** (custom CR-6 cleric), **CM-zargon-cultist` — actually CM-zargon-cult-fanatic** (custom CR-1).
