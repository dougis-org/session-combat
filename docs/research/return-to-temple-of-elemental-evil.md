# Return to the Temple of Elemental Evil — Research

## Adventure structure

3rd edition D&D sequel to T1-4 *The Temple of Elemental Evil*, published 2001 by Wizards of the Coast. Author: Monte Cook. Levels 4–14. Set in Greyhawk, 12 years after the original module, with the cult of the Elder Elemental Eye rebuilding at the Temple of All-Consumption. The adventure re-centers the cult around **Tharizdun** (the Elder Elemental Eye is revealed as an aspect of Tharizdun). 192 pages, 8 chapters in 3 parts.

| Part | Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | Hommlet & Moathouse Return | 4–6 | Hommlet / Moathouse (excavated cult site) |
| 2 | 3 | Crater Ridge Mines | 7–9 | Crater Ridge Mines |
| 2 | 4 | Outer Fane | 9–11 | Temple of All-Consumption outer |
| 2 | 5 | Inner Fane | 11–13 | Temple of All-Consumption inner |
| 3 | 6–8 | Temple of Elemental Evil Finale | 12–14 | Original Temple of Elemental Evil (restored) |

Final villain: Tharizdun's priest-king Imix (Prince of Evil Fire Elementals) + archomental manifestations of elemental princes.

## Encounter plan (sample set-pieces)

### Ch 1–2 — Hommlet & Moathouse
- **Cult Spies Beneath Hommlet.** New cult of the Elder Elemental Eye has infiltrated the town. Monsters: **CM-temple-of-elemental-evil-cult-spy** (custom CR-4 cultist), bugbear assassin.

### Ch 3 — Crater Ridge Mines
- **Earth Cult Mining Operation.** Crater Ridge has been converted to elemental earth node mining; dwarves and earth elementals work for the cult. Monsters: earth elemental (SRD), **CM-earth-cultist-acolyte** (custom), dwarven cult conscripts.

### Ch 4 — Outer Fane
- **Temple of All-Consumption — Outer Courts.** Gnolls, bugbear warriors, and lesser earth cultists defend the perimeter. Monsters: gnoll (SRD), gnoll pack lord (SRD), bugbear chief (SRD), **CM-temple-priest** (custom CR-8 cleric).

### Ch 5 — Inner Fane
- **Cult Inner Sanctum.** Bound earth elemental, summoned gricks, beholder-eye tyrant guarding the inner temple. Monsters: **CM-temple-priest** (high CR), grick (SRD), earth elemental (SRD), beholder (SRD).

### Ch 6–8 — Temple of Elemental Evil Finale
- **Imix, Prince of Evil Fire Elementals + Zuggtmoy / Iuz Convergence.** All four elemental princes (fire = Imix, earth = Ogrémoch, water = Olhydra, air = Yan-C-Bin) manifest. Final boss fight. Monsters: **CM-imix-prince-of-fire** (custom CR-21 archomental), elemental myrmidons (SRD), **CM-zuggtmoy-return-from-original** (custom).
