# Vault of the Drow (D-series) — Research

## Adventure structure

The D-series (D1 *Descent into the Depths of the Earth* → D2 *Shrine of the Kuo-Toa* → D3 *Vault of the Drow*) was originally published as three standalone 1e AD&D modules in 1978, then compiled as D1-2 *Descent into the Depths of the Earth* (1981) and reprinted with D3 *Vault of the Drow* (1981). Set in the World of Greyhawk, levels 9–14. This is the third stage of the GDQ1-7 *Queen of the Spiders* supermodule, focused on introducing the drow as iconic monsters. The Underdark here introduced: troglodytes, jermlaine, svirfneblin (deep gnomes), mind flayers, drow.

For G5, treat as a **4-chapter sampling** of the D-series proper (without the G1-G3 giant stages):

| Ch | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Descent into the Depths of the Earth (D1) | 9–12 | Underdark (vast caverns) |
| 2 | Shrine of the Kuo-Toa (D2) | 10–12 | Underdark (kuo-toa territory) |
| 3 | Vault of the Drow (D3) | 11–13 | Erelhei-Cinlu (drow city) |
| 4 | Beyond the Vault (D3 epilogue / astral gate) | 13–14 | Astral gate to Lolth's layer (links to Q1) |

## Encounter plan (4-chapter mega-dungeon sampling)

### Ch 1 (D1) — Descent into the Depths of the Earth
- **Drow Patrol Ambush.** Drow fugitives from G3 hall are being chased through the Underdark by mind flayers and wererats. Monsters: **CM-drow-patrol** (CR-8), mind flayer (SRD), wererat (SRD), quaggoth slave.

### Ch 2 (D2) — Shrine of the Kuo-Toa
- **The Kuo-Toan Rogue Monitor.** A kuo-toan guide offers to lead the party across a river, for a price. The party then enters kuo-toan territory ruled by Priest-Prince Va-Guulgh. Monsters: kuo-toan (SRD), **CM-priest-prince-va-guulgh** (CR-8 kuo-toa cleric), sahuagin scouts, **CM-svirfneblin-deep-gnome** (resistance allies).

### Ch 3 (D3) — Vault of the Drow
- **Erelhei-Cinlu (Drow City).** The vast subterranean city of the drow; first major appearance of driders, Lolth's temple, and the drow matriarchy. Monsters: drow (SRD), drider (SRD), **CM-eclavdra-priestess-of-Lolth** (custom CR-13), quaggoth (SRD analog).

### Ch 4 (D3 epilogue / astral gate) — Beyond the Vault
- **Astral Gate to the Demonweb Pits.** Hidden portal leads to Lolth's realm. Monsters: phase spider (SRD), **CM-lolth-demon-queen** (custom CR-26+ demon lord), drider temple guards (SRD).
