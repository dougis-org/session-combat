# The Dark of Hot Springs Island — Research

## Adventure structure

Third-party **system-neutral** sandbox by Kevin Crawford, published by Sine Nomine Publishing (2015). Designed to be compatible with any OSR / D&D-adjacent system. Levels roughly 1–10. Set on a massive volcanic island in uncharted waters — a sandbox adventure with high lethality, exploration focus, and a strange "color" magic system based on combining colors. The island's inhabitants are savage, desperate, and the island itself hides ancient technological ruins.

For G5 we treat it as a **4-chapter regional sandbox sampling**:

| Ch | Title | Levels | Region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Coastal North | 1–3 | North island (coastal, fishing villages) |
| 2 | The Volcanic Center | 3–6 | Island interior (volcano, ruined cities) |
| 3 | The Far South | 6–8 | South island (mysterious ruins) |
| 4 | The Sea Caves & Sunless Reach | 8–10 | Under-island volcanic caves |

The campaign is system-neutral — exact monster stats vary by host system. Common OSR / D&D conversions exist.

## Encounter plan (sample key set-pieces)

### Ch 1 — The Coastal North
- **Raider Ambush from the Hex.** Hex-scarred raiders attack a fishing village. Monsters: **CM-hot-springs-raider` (custom CR-2)**, **CM-shark-man-jaguar` (custom CR-3)**, **CM-megalodon-stalker` (custom CR-5)**.

### Ch 2 — The Volcanic Center
- **Slag-Walker in the Lava Fields.** An elemental-touched beast. Monsters: **CM-slag-walker` (custom CR-6)**, **CM-pit-stalker` (custom CR-4)**, **CM-volcano-troll** (custom CR-5).

### Ch 3 — The Far South (Ruined Cities)
- **Lizardman Cult in the Black City.** The far south has technologically-advanced ruins overrun by savage humanoids. Monsters: **CM-black-city-lizardman-cultist` (custom CR-3)**, **CM-arcology-guardian-robot` (custom CR-7 construct)**, **CM-floating-eye-stalker` (custom CR-6 aberration)**.

### Ch 4 — The Sea Caves & Sunless Reach
- **Leviathan in the Sunless Sea.** Final boss, an elder beast of the undersea caves. Monsters: **CM-sunlessless-leviathan` (custom CR-10)**, **CM-deep-one-hybrid` (use SRD aboleth or custom CR-5)**, **CM-eel-shark-stalker` (custom CR-4)**.

## Confidence notes

- **Chapter structure**: LOW-MEDIUM confidence. The campaign is a sandbox, not a linear path; chapter structure is inferred from regional divisions.
- **Encounter structure**: LOW confidence. The system-neutral book describes monsters narratively; exact stat blocks depend on host system.
- **Monsters**: HIGH confidence that Hot Springs Island needs many custom stat blocks (it's not a D&D module; monsters are unique to the setting). Authors using D&D would need to convert from the narrative descriptions.
- **Status**: System-neutral OSR; conversion to 5e requires building custom stat blocks from narrative descriptions. Sine Nomine's other titles (Stars Without Number) are also popular OSR systems.
