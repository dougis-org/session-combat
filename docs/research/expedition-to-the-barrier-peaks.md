# Expedition to the Barrier Peaks — Research

## Adventure structure

S3 module (1980) by Gary Gygax. 1e AD&D. Levels 8–12. Set on a downed spaceship in the Barrier Peaks mountain range of Greyhawk. Originally played at Origins II 1976 as a tournament scenario to introduce D&D players to Metamorphosis Alpha (a science-fiction RPG). The adventure is a **science-fantasy crossover**: D&D characters on a spaceship filled with robots, vegepygmies, and futuristic technology. Published 1980 with 5-part adventure guide plus separate illustration booklet.

Ranked 5th greatest D&D adventure of all time by *Dungeon* magazine (2004). A 5e remake was published in *Quests from the Infinite Staircase* (2024) and in *Original Adventures Reincarnated #3* (Goodman Games, 2019).

For G5 we treat the S3 module as a **6-section mega-dungeon sampling** (the spaceship's distinct zones):

| Sect | Title | Levels | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Crew's Quarters | 8–9 | Dormitory and wardroom |
| 2 | The Lounge Area | 9 | Dining Servo Robot zone |
| 3 | The Gardens & Menagerie | 9–10 | Alien flora and fauna |
| 4 | Activity Deck — Sports & Combat | 10 | Boxing & karate robot gym |
| 5 | Loading Area | 10–12 | Cargo bay + exit |
| 6 | Boss: Froghemoth | 11–12 | Central chamber (froghemoth arena) |

## Encounter plan (6-section mega-dungeon sampling)

### Sect 1 — Crew's Quarters (Vegepygmy Territory)
- **Vegepygmy Commune.** Short plant humanoids have taken over crew quarters. Monsters: vegepygmy (SRD), **CM-vegepygmy-chief** (custom CR-2), repair robot (custom).

### Sect 2 — Lounge Area
- **Dining Servo Robot Encounter.** Still-functional robot serves moldy-poison food. Monsters: **CM-dining-servo-robot** (custom CR-3 construct), flying automaton (use SRD homunculus or construct analog).

### Sect 3 — Gardens & Menagerie
- **The Bunnyoid on the Stump.** Cute horned rabbit on tree stump with fanged, tentacled roots. Monsters: **CM-bunnyoid-stump` — actually called bunnyoid ambush plant** (custom CR-5 monstrous plant), **CM-froghemoth` actually** (custom CR-10 mega-monster, key boss).

### Sect 4 — Activity Deck (Boxing & Karate Robots)
- **Boxing Robot vs. Karate Robot Match.** Robots robots' combatants face off — heroes can manipulate them to fight each other. Monsters: **CM-boxing-robot** (custom CR-4 construct), **CM-karate-robot** (custom CR-5 construct).

### Sect 5 — Loading Area
- **Final Escape Point.** Heroes reach the loading area, can escape the ship. Monsters: **CM-fighter-interceptor-droid** (custom CR-6), security drones (use SRD construct analog).

### Sect 6 — Froghemoth Boss
- **The Froghemoth.** Alien frog-like giant with tentacles and three eyes. Monsters: **CM-froghemoth` actually** (custom CR-10 mega-monster).
